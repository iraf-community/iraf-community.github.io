Hi,<br>

For what its worth, the message is printed when the math routine that solves the equations returns an error.  The common reasons are a singular matrix or no solution.  The database has a value STATUS with an error code that might provide a more specific answer.   As to why your particular equations failed to yield a solution that depends on the functions used and the data.  I can't help much more.<br>
