MSCCMATCH only affects the low order terms of a WCS; namely, the origin, rotation, linear scales, and angle between the axes (skew).  To do a full solution you will have to use mscfinder.msctpeak.  This is a task based on a package called finder.  There is no help page for msctpeak but the interactive help (typing ? when you get the blinking image cursor) is fairly helpful.  You might look at<br>
<br>
<a href="http://iraf.noao.edu/projects/ccdmosaic/astrometry/astrom.html">http://iraf.noao.edu/projects/ccdmosaic/astrometry/astrom.html</a><br>
<br>
For some information about WCS.<br>

Frank Valdes
