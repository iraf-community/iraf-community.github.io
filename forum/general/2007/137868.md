**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jul 27, 2007

{% raw %}
<div>
In the case of MATCH, and generally, you can't logically and/or params  as in your example.  To OR:  run the task twice.  To AND:  pipe the results of one match into another, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; match (&quot;foo&quot;, &quot;data&quot;) | match (&quot;bar&quot;, &quot;STDIN&quot;, .&gt; &quot;datalist2&quot;)</code></pre><br>
<br>
Otherwise it's up to the task whether things like this are supported directly.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
