<div>
The results might in fact be correct, it's impossible to tell without seeing the image header and/or WCS keywords, it all depend on where the CRPIX reference pixel is defined and the CRVAL values at that point (plus the CD matrix defining the scale/rotation).  <br>
<br>
An alternative way to do this is with the WCSCTRAN task where the 'inwcs' is "world" and the 'outwcs' param is 'logical'.   The 'physical' system will tell you where the point is in the image independent of whether it was copied out of another image section.  Post the image header if you still have problems.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike

<br>

If your target is obviously in the image then the WCS may be wrong, or else are you sure your target position is also in ICRS??<br>
