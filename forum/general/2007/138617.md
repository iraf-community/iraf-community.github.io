<div>
It works simple script ;<br>
<br>
list = "datalist"<br>
while (fscan (list, s1) !=EOF) {<br>
    imgets (s1, "DATE-OBS")<br>
    hedit (s1, "TIME-OBS", substr(imgets.value,12,19), add+, ver-)<br>
}
