<div>
Of course not 10 minutes after I posted this I got a fix:<br>
<br>
(1) I installed the 32-bit libraries for RedHat Enterprise V4 (xorg-x11-libs-6.8.2-1.EL.19.i386.rpm) which I got from the RedHat Network.  The .rpm file installed just fine, despite my thinking that it would conflict with the existing 64-bin libraries.<br>
<br>
(2) That gave me the "no available ptys" error, so I downloaded the patch from <a href="http://iraf.noao.edu/new_stuff/xgterm_fedora.html">http://iraf.noao.edu/new_stuff/xgterm_fedora.html</a>.<br>
<br>
This worked just fine, and now I'm up-and-running.<br>
<br>
- Jonathan
