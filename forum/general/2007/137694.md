**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jul 08, 2007

{% raw %}
<div>
Garbage output on the screen always means that there is a mis-match between the type of terminal you are using, and the type of terminal IRAF thinks you're using.  Note that for graphics your choice is either XTerm or XGterm, when you do a MKIRAF you are asked for the terminal type and you should reply 'xterm' or 'xgterm' appropriately.  Gnome-terminals, rxvt, kde-term, cannot be used -- you must actually login to iraf through a running xterm/xgterm.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
