# fixpix "MWCS: dimension mismatch" error

**mikewong** wrote on Mar 09, 2007

{% raw %}
<div>
[reposted from the buglog comment]<br>
<br>
i'm using IRAF 2.13-BETA2 on a macintel and i could not get fixpix to work. it kept giving the same error:<br>
<br>
   Warning: MWCS: dimension mismatch (mw_gltermd)<br>
<br>
i searched and found buglog #458 (<a href="http://iraf.net/article.php?story=7337)">http://iraf.net/article.php?story=7337)</a>. indeed, i had been supplying fixpix with a dimensionally reduced image. i deleted the WCSDIM and WAXMAP01 keywords from the inputs to fixpix, and then the task worked perfectly.
