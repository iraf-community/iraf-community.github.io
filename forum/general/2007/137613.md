**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 20, 2007

<div>
You mean in the CL?  Simple, just prepend the expression with an '=', e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; = (4861/5000 -1)*299793
-299793</code></pre><br>
<br>
Note you'll need to be explicit about using floating-point numbers (calculations are done internally as double precision.  FWIW, you can use the same trick to evaluate sexigesimal values as well:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; = 12:34:56.7
12.582416666667</code></pre><br>
<br>
Remember to multiply RA by 15 8-)<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
