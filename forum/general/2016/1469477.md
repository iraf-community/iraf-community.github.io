**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Nov 14, 2016

{% raw %}
<div>
<br>
                The 'm' key does straight image statistics on a rectangular region, however the 'a' key fits a surface when computing the sky background within the object aperture.  The two results should be close but not necessarily equal.<br>
