**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 08, 2008

{% raw %}
<div>
If the images all have a valid WCS, then have a look at the WREGISTER task as a way to register the images, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; wregister uncut.fits cut.fits newcut.fits</code></pre><br>
<br>
The image will be trimmed and aligned as needed.  See also other tasks in the IMAGES.IMMATCH package if this doesn't do it.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
