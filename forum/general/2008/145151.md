<div>
There are several ways depending on what you're trying to do:  The LISTPIX task (with wcs=world) will list the wavelength vs flux pairs as a text file and you could select from that list.  SCOPY will let you similarly copy out a small section of the spectra and optionally rebin to specific start/end values, combined with LISTPIX this would give you a more precise list you could sum to get an integrated flux.  Lastly,  if what you want is some bit of functionality found in SPLOT, see the BPLOT task as a way to do that non-interively.<br>
<br>
-Mike
