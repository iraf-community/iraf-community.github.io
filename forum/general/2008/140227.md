<div>
Unless I'm not remembering things correctly:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cdelt1  = cd1_1
cdelt2  = cd2_2
crota1  = atan2 ( cd2_1, cd1_1)
crota2  = atan2 (-cd1_2, cd2_2)
</code></pre><br>
<br>
Near the poles or when the x-axis is the Dec/Lat these equations change and there are special conditions, but in most cases i think these work.<br>
<br>
For reference, the CROT/CDELT to CD-matrix transform is:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
CD1_1 = CDELT1 * cos (CROTA2)
CD1_2 = -CDELT2 * sin (CROTA2)
CD2_1 = CDELT1 * sin (CROTA2)
CD2_2 = CDELT2 * cos (CROTA2) 
</code></pre><br>
<br>
Hope this helps, hope others can offer needed corrections/elaboration.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
