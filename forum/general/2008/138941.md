Hello,<br>
<br>
Overscan corrections are used to track very short term electronic drifts; on the time scale of an exposure.  Bias or dark frames are also overscan corrected to leave any more stable 2D pattern of dark counts or electronic zero point variations for removal from the overscan corrected science exposures.<br>
<br>
Yes, it is generally the case that one uses dark calibrations in place of bias or zero frame calibrations.<br>
<br>
So you should use overscan and dark calibrations.<br>
