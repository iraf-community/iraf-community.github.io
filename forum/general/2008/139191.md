**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Mar 19, 2008

<div>
DS9 is not an IRAF task, so you'll need to escape it to start, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; !ds9 &amp;</code></pre><br>
<br>
This requires that DS9 be in your path, or that you specify the full path to the binary.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
