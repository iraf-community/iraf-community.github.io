The task that would probably of most use is imexpr.  The actual expression you use might be based on the equation you noted.  My main concern would be what does "calibrated" mean.  If any scaling is done, say normalizing to unit exposure time or gain or dividing by an unnormalized flat field) or the units are change from adu to something else then you need to figure out how to convert the detection gain and readnoise.<br>
<br>
I notice you did not have the gain in your equation.  What you show would only be correct if the gain is one (i.e. the signal is in terms of photons).  The readout noise would also need to be in photons though typically it is in measure electrons.<br>
<br>
I hope this helps,<br>
Frank Valdes
