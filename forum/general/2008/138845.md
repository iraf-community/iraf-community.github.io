<div>
Does the task actually have parameters, i.e. any variable declared before the 'begin' statement or as an argument in the 'procedure' is considered a paramter.  If not, the the task should be declared as<br>
<br>
<pre><code> 
task $ap_pierside_obhead_v_1 = /home/piverson/research/ap_pierside_obhead_v_1.cl </code></pre><br>
<br>
where the '$' indicates the task has no params.  You might also try to unlearn it in case an earlier declaration or version of the script is being pulled from your uparm directory.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
