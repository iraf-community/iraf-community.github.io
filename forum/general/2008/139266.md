<div>
Hi Owen,<br>
<br>
In general the more recent version would be the one most likely to be correct since any identified bugs would have been fixed.  It is rare that change would actually make things worse.<br>
<br>
However, SPLOT has evolved very little in quite some time (I don't think there have been any changes between the versions you mention).  To say anything specific would require knowing exactly what you did (there are several ways to measure lines in SPLOT and some depend, sometimes sensitively, on cursor input).<br>
<br>
Let me know if I can shed any more light on your problem.<br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
