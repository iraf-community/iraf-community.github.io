The warning about the graphics overlay is normal and can be safely ignored.  The problem with entering commands may simply be a matter of window focus, i.e. you may need to click on the border of the graphics window in order that keystrokes will be sent to that window.  If you have to normally click-to-focus then this is your problem.  <br>
<br>
Otherwise, do other interactive tasks like IMPLOT work normally?  Does the CL command prompt come back in the text window or does the task appear to be waiting for cursor input?<br>
