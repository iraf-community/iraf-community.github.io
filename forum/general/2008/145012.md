The usual source of this error is a WCS that is 3D even though the image may be 2D; i.e. the 3rd axis is considered to be one pixel high.  You could post the header listings and I could tell you what is wrong but I think you do understand that it is caused by the way the WCS are being represented (which may be technically correct but is not expected by the IRAF tasks).<br>
