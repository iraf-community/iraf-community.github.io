Hi,<br>
<br>
You refer to using trimming to remove the overscan.  Typically this is done using CCDPROC.  I can't say why you get the error but I suggest you try the following.  Do your trimming as you have been doing.  Do the same trim for both your spectrum and your arc.  If you want flip the spectrum at the same time you can do it with an image section.  For example:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
imcopy raw[1024:33,*] trimmed
wcsreset trimmed wcs=physical
</code></pre><br>
<br>
The second line may help solve any confusions later.  This tells IRAF to forget that the original raw image was trimmed and flipped.<br>
<br>
Maybe this will solve your problems.<br>
<br>

Frank Valdes
