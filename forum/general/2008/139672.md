<div>
In general the 32-bit IRAF binaries will work on 64-bit systems, the exceptions being that one cannot (yet) compile on the 64-bit system and you may need to install 32-bit compatability libraries to support some things.  64-bit filesystems have sometimes been a problem in the past, but a lot depends on the linux distribution.<br>
<br>
A general introdiuction can be found at  <a href="http://mjhutchinson.com/journal/2006/11/05/install_iraf_on_ubuntu_edgy_amd64">http://mjhutchinson.com/journal/2006/11/05/install_iraf_on_ubuntu_edgy_amd64</a> or you can search the forums here for when that didn't cover the topic completely.  Full 64-bit support is still pending.<br>
<br>
-Mike
