**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on May 27, 2008

{% raw %}
<div>
My first thought is that you need to apply the Jan14 critical update mentioned on the homepage.  This fixes a bug affecting image sections that might explain what you are seeing.<br>
<br>
Second, are the x_orig/y_orig variables real or integer?  If they are real the image section you create might include the decimal place and so I'd also print out the section to be sure it is what you expect.<br>
<br>
<blockquote>ps: of cause it would also solve my problem if their is an easy way to "enlarge" a given image (where the new pixels all have zero values)</blockquote><br>
<br>
Have a look at the IMLINTRAN task.  For example, to copy the dev$pix test image to the lower-left corner of a larger image you would do something like<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imlintran dev$pix foo.fits 0. 0. 1. 1. xin=1 yin=1 xout=1 yout=1 ncols=2048 nlines=2048</code></pre><br>
<br>
Hope this helps.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
