<div>
This reminded me of a problem I was having last summer.  I posted the solution on <a href="http://iparrizar.mnstate.edu/~juan/urania/2007/08/09/hint-resolving-xgterm-problems-outside-of-iraf/">http://iparrizar.mnstate.edu/~juan/urania/2007/08/09/hint-resolving-xgterm-problems-outside-of-iraf/</a>.  In a nutshell. I would check your login.cl file and any other cl scripts you run in IRAF and make sure they DO not execute a <br>
<br>
<pre><code>stty xterm</code></pre><br>
<br>
I found that replacing it with <br>
<br>
<pre><code>stty xgterm</code></pre><br>
<br>
fixed everything.
</div>
{% endraw %}
