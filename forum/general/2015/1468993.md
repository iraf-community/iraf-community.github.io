**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 19, 2015

{% raw %}
<div>
<br>
                See the help pages for both DISPTRANS and SPLOT for their discussion of velocity units.  For velocity you need to also specify the velocity zero point, e.g.<br>
<br>
    cl&gt; disptrans old.fits new.fits "km/s 1 micron"<br>
<br>
would define 1 micron as the zero point.  Note the quotes around the entire unit string so it isn't taken as separate arguments.<br>
</div>
{% endraw %}
