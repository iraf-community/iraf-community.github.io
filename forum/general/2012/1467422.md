**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 27, 2012

{% raw %}
<div>
The answer depends on the task so I'd suggest consulting individual help pages first.  For SPLOT there is the options="histogram" parameter, for IMEXAM see the HIMEXAM pset parameters, and AFAIK IMPLOT doesn't do histograms at all.<br>
<br>
In general, tasks that use the GTOOLS interface (i.e. the ":/&lt;cmd&gt;" have a ":/type histogram" option, but this interface isn't universal.
