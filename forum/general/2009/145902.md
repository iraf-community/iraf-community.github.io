**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 07, 2009

<div>
A 'command not found' may just mean the 'local bin directory' where you installed the IRAF commands isn't in your path.  Normally this is /usr/local/bin but depends on how you obtained/installed the system (e.g. the SciSoft distro, from this site etc).  Anyway, you need to adjust the path defined in the .cshrc or .bashrc file depending on your shell to include the iraf commands directory.<br>
<br>
-Mike
