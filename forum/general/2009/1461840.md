<div>
SLALIB is a library for compiled tasks.  If all you want is to convert an RA to radians you can do it in the CL directly, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cl&gt; =rad (ra * 15.0)
cl&gt; = (ra * 15) * (PI / 180.)
</code></pre><br>
<br>
These two are equivalent, there is also a complementary deg() function to convert to degrees.  The CL handles sexagesimal directly, but you still need to multiple RA by 15 to get degrees as a floating point value.<br>
<br>
-Mike
