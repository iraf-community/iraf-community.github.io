<div>
A simpler way to add extensions is with RENAME, see Example 5 of the help page:<br>
<br>
<blockquote><br>
    5. Add a ".fits" extension to all files in a directory.<br>
    <br>
        cl&gt; rename im00* fits field=extn<br>
</blockquote><br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
