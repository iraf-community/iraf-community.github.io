**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Sep 13, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
In the "command mode" syntax you use for the IMEXPR line, there should be no commas between the parameters.  It is "program mode" where you use braces around the parameters and commas between them.  Try e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>imexpr (expr=&quot;...&quot;, output=&quot;blank.fits&quot;, dims=&quot;256,256&quot;, .....)</code></pre><br>
<br>
I think this may also explain your other IMINSERT posting, I'll have to check whether this should be producing a syntax error but I don't think it has anything to do with Snow Leopard.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
