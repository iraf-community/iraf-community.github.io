**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Dec 01, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
Try using the "program mode" syntax, i.e.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
imstat (s1, &gt;&gt; &quot;text.out&quot;)
</code></pre><br>
<br>
What you have now is called "compute mode" and is the style you would use at the CL prompt.  I thought I tested this before my earlier reply and it worked either way, but if you are really using  procedure script and your post was just a snippet then that would explain the error.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
