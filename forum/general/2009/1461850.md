<div>
Assuming you simply want to replace pixels, you can use image sections in both the input and output image with a simple IMCOPY, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imcopy dev$pix[301:400,301:400] foo.fits[101:200,101:200]</code></pre><br>
<br>
You'll need to be sure to get the section right based in the image size.<br>
<br>
Otherwise, IMCOMBINE can also do this and can do more than simply replace the pixels.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
