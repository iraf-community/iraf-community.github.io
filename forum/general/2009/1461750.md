{% raw %}
<div>
Hello James,<br>
<br>
Yes a screen flat is the same as a dome flat.  A sky flat are exposures taken at twilight where any sources that shine through are hopefully removed when multiple exposures are stacked.  A sky flat, also called a twilight flat, may do a better job of illumination correction but may also have more problems with gradients from the setting sun and sources.<br>

Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
