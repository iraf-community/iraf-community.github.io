I've written a procedure script which uses the 'fields' task. I can't make it stop asking me to confirm the lines to be extracted. This is how the 'fields' parameters are set:<br>
<br>
fields.mode = 'hl'<br>
fields.files = 'file1'<br>
fields.fields = "1-2"<br>
fields.lines = "1-5"<br>
fields ('file2')<br>
<br>
Every time I'm required to confirm the "lines" parameter, even though it's already in the parameters.<br>
It's a minor problem, but I'd really like to fix it, so the script won't stop and wait for confirmation.<br>
<br>

<div>
I think you mean it keeps prompting you for the 'fields' param??  If so, you can make this one parameter hidden by adding the line<br>
<br>
<pre><code>fields.fields.p_mode=&quot;h&quot;</code></pre><br>
<br>
Or else just supply the param when you make the call, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>fields (&quot;file2&quot;, &quot;1-2&quot;)  </code></pre><br>
<br>
-Mike
