**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Dec 26, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
The image sections specified by BIASSEC et al are given as "a,b:c,d" and not the expected "a:b,c:d" format, i.e. the comma and colon are switched.  See e.g. the comment for the CCDSEC keyword.
</div>
{% endraw %}
