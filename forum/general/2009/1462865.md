<div>
Hi Julia,<br>
<br>
You sent me a separate email with a header listing of the extension.  I am going to reply through the forum.<br>
<br>
The information you sent indicates to me that the spectrum is in a table.  Currently the IRAF spectral formats supported by tasks such as SPLOT consist only of images.  So you would have to convert the data into a spectral image or use tools intended for tables.<br>
<br>
I found the following link with some format information<br>
<br>
<a href="http://tkserver.keck.hawaii.edu/tksurvey/data_products/type.html">http://tkserver.keck.hawaii.edu/tksurvey/data_products/type.html</a><br>
<br>
So you can use IGI or SGRAPH to plot things from the table format.  You can dump the wavelength and flux data with tdump (or tprint) and import it into and IRAF spectrum image with RSPECTEXT.<br>
<br>
Good luck,<br>
Frank Valdes<br>
<br>
P.S.  The error message does sound like things which have been noted earlier in the forums having to do with problems with the spectral WCS.  However, in this case the error is really due to the data not being an image.<br>
