<div>
The CL assumes anything starting with a number must be a number, in general IRAF requires that filenames/images begin with a letter.  If you must use dgits in the file names, then you need to quote them in the CL, eg.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imstat &quot;001.fits&quot;</code></pre><br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
