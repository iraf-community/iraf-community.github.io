**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on May 06, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
That message usually means the scan to the variable failed, i.e. either the fscan() in the loop didn't read anything into 'imname', or you're running something like "foo() | scan(str)" but the foo() didn't produce any output so the scan didn't load 'str' and you later tried to use it.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
