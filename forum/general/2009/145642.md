**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Feb 25, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
Tryusing an image section so the WSPEC task sees only one dimension, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; wspectext  foo.fits[*,1] STDOUT</code></pre><br>
<br>
The IMSLICE task will generally reduce the dimensionality of an image, off-hand though I'm not sure what it would do to the spectral WCS however.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
