**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Mar 11, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
The region stuff is internal to DS9 and is apparently disabled when the task goes into 'image cursor' mode.  There isn't any way to reset this in DS9 that I know of, and certainly not in IMEXAM, other that to quit cursor mode temporarily.<br>
<br>
Note that XImtool also has a way to draw markers on the display and these remain sensitive even in cursor mode.<br>
<br>
-Mike
