I'm not sure what is wrong with mscjoin.  As a first step, I have put the bad pixel masks used by the mosaic3 pipeline at NOAO at<br>
<br>
ftp://ftp.noao.edu/pub/valdes/Mosaic3/<br>
<br>
These are also one file per CCD, which is the way the pipeline works.  I assume you want to make an MEF so that you can use mscred.ccdproc?  Mscjoin is just a simple task based on imcopy.  You could try and do it your self with something like<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
imcopy ccd1 mef[ccd1,append]
imcopy ccd2 mef[ccd2,append]
...
 </code></pre><br>
<br>
I can't remember immediately what the extension names are for the mosaic3 data. <br>
