<br>
XGterm (and XImtool) are 32-bit binaries and so you'll need to install the compatibility libraries for 32-bit binaries, including any special libs for X11.  The tasks cannot simply be recompiled from source on a 64-bit system and no port is planned due to the amount of work required.<br>
