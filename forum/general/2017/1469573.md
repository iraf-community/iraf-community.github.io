<br>
I think the problem here is related to your friend's language settings. Sorry if it's inconvenient, but if you change your terminal to work in English then at least the scripts should run. Try unsetting any environment variables that begin with "LC_", eg. as follows:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>unset `env | grep "^LC_" | sed -e 's|=.*$||'`</code></pre><br>
<br>
Hope that helps.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
<br>
James.<br>
