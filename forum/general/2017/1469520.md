**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jan 27, 2017

<div>
<br>
                The answer may depend on your DS9 version, and saord@cfa.harvard.edu would be the place to ask about specific DS9 options.<br>
<br>
However, the image being displayed by IRAF is centered in the framebuffer defined by the 'stdimage' variable.  For the standard 512x512 dev$pix image this means that if stdimage=imt800 the image is centered in an 800x800 frame where the area outside the image has zero values and is rendered as black.  Now if you stretch the DS9 window so the display area is bigger than that, then the area outside of the *frame buffer* is white, but that's a matter of how DS9 renders the frame buffer and not how IRAF displayed the image.  You can use a smaller frame buffer and/or turn on the 'fill' parameter to fill the frame buffer, but that won't eliminate the white area.  To do that the only option I know of is to make the ds9 window smaller.<br>
