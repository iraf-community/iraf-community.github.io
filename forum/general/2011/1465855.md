Just to let you know that the color postscript in stsdas and tables extern packages needs to have symbolic links to the "bin": <pre><code>cd /iraf/iraf/extern/tables
ln -s bin bin.linux
cd ../stsdas
ln -s bin bin.linux</code></pre><br>
and then, on the plot<pre><code>:.snap psidump</code></pre><br>
in cl<pre><code>gflush</code></pre><br>
and in /tmp search for psidumpNNNNN.ps color PS.
