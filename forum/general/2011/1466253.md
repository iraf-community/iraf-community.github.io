The rms in the earlier part is the rms per star and is often dominated by the uncertainties in the catalog coordinates.  What you are asking about is the uncertainty in the fit.  If the errors are truely random then I believe that dividing the provided rms per star by the square root of the number of stars used in the fit is effectively what you are looking for.  Of course this is a purely formal estimate.<br>
