It sounds like what you want is to fit a Voigt profile, which is a combination of Gaussian and Lorentzian profiles (see e.g. wikipedia).  Both SPLOT and the FITPROFS task can be used to fit this type of profile, I can't really comment on how you might determine how much of the profile is due to pressure effects.
