A 'memory corrupted' usually indicates a bug in the program, but it could be one triggered by something in the data or an unusual combination of parameters.  There's not much we can do to fix it without being able to reproduce the problem locally, or other isolating the cause and finding some other workaround.<br>
<br>
Could you provide the parameters you used (e.g. a "dpar &lt;task&gt;" listing for all the tasks and psets you modified) and a sample image that demonstrates the problem and upload them to the anonftp at <a href="ftp://iraf.noao.edu/pub">ftp://iraf.noao.edu/pub</a>?  If we can trap the problem we'll fix it for the next release and provide you with an updated binary until then.<br>
