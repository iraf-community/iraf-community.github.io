<div>
What system?<br>
<br>
On Mac systems with Mac apps like Terminal you use Cmd-c and Cmd-v, however cut-n-paste between a Terminal.app and and xterm doesn't work (Google for FAQs), or at least not reliably.  On some linux systems it's Ctrl-Shift-c and Ctrl-Shift-v, or menu options only.  As always, a lot of this is configurable in various ways (e.g. programming function keys, remapping the Caps-Lock/Control keys, etc).
