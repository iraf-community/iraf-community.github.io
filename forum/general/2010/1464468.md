<div>
Does "help longslit" help? Assuming you're talking about a single slit spectrum, you should be able to do identify-&gt;reidentify-&gt;fitcoords-&gt;transform to get your 2D spectrum on a linear grid, for example. You might find the following guide useful.<br>
<br>
  <a href="http://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/ftp/iraf/docs/spect.ps.Z">http://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/ftp/iraf/docs/spect.ps.Z</a><br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
<br>
James.

since all standards stars I observed are at the same position on the slit I cannot use the transform routine (anyway there is no noticeable geometrical distortion in the spectra).</blockquote><br>
I'm not 100% sure what you're saying, but I believe you can run transform after doing identify and fitcoords on an arc, even if you haven't run identify in the spatial direction.<br>
<br>
<blockquote>I used identify, reidentify and then fitcoords. However, it's not clear for me when do you have your images wavelength calibrated. Do I have to run refspectra and dispcor routines as usual?</blockquote><br>
The images should have the correct wavelength calibration in the header after you run either transform or dispcor.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
<br>
James.
