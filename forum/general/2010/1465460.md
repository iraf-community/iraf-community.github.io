**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Oct 13, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
You specify overwrite mode as e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>foo.fits[1,overwrite+]</code></pre><br>
<br>
See also <a href="http://iraf.net/irafdocs/fits_userguide/">http://iraf.net/irafdocs/fits_userguide/</a>
</div>
{% endraw %}
