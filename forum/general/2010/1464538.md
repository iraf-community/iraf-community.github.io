**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 09, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
Try something like<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cl&gt; listpix dev$pix[1:10,1:10] verb- | fields STDIN 3 | average STDIN
</code></pre><br>
<br>
The sum of the pixels in the section will be in the 'sum' parameter of the AVERAGE task or you can multiple the average times the number of samples that appear on the output.
</div>
{% endraw %}

<div>
<blockquote>what is 'dev$pix' ?</blockquote><br>
<br>
The standard test image distributed with the system.<br>
<br>
<blockquote>verb- says do not list the pixel values ?</blockquote><br>
<br>
See the help page, it just supresses the title line not the pixels.<br>
<br>
<blockquote>what is ' | fields STDIN 3' ? especially number 3?</blockquote><br>
<br>
The example pipes the output of LISTPIX to the FIELDS task where column 3 is selected, that then gets piped to the AVERAGE task to do the sum.
</div>

<div>
SMP,<br>
If you do an imstat file.fits[x1:x2,y1:y2]. it will print the number of pixels and the average. Multiply the two and you will have the sum of counts in all those pixels.<br>
N.B. the number of pixels in x1:x2 is NOT x2-x1. Eg. 1:10 is 10 pixels,<br>
10-1=9.<br>
<br>
Jim
