The task "PHOT" has output format  %-6d for ID.  That means it can only process<br>

<br>

**Phillip Warner** wrote on May 05, 2006

{% raw %}
<div>
Hi Ping,<br>
<br>
You will need to edit noao/digiphot/apphot/aplib/apwres1.x; you can change the formatting for the ID column to -7d (i.e., in ID_FSTR and ID_WSTR).  As the lines are 80 characters long, you'll also need to sacrifice some precision (one digit in this case) or a character in another parameter.<br>
<br>
After that, you'll need to recompile the digiphot package.<br>
<br>
Hope that helps.
</div>
{% endraw %}
