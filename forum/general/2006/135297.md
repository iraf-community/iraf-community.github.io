Situation resolved.<br>
<br>
For the sake of anyone running RedHat.<br>
<br>
To figure out what package is associated with a given file, use <strong>rpm -qf filename</strong><br>
<br>
On the 64-bit machines, where one needs the 32-bit support, you can then<br>
get the 32-bit packages with:  <strong>up2date --arch=i386 &lt;packagename&gt;</strong><br>
<br>
-- Ed
