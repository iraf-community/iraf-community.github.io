<br>
IMCOMBINE is able to work on non-2D images, however IMARITH cannot.  You probably have either a 1-D or 3-D image (i.e. an image 100x100x1 is considered 3-D].  You can use the IMHEADER task to check the image, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imhead moon1.fits long+</code></pre><br>
<br>
If it's a 3-D image then something like<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imarith moon1[*,*,1] + moon[*,*,1] newmoon</code></pre><br>
<br>
will use the image section [*,*,1] to extract the 2-D band from the image, or else do the same but IMCOPY the section to a new image.<br>
