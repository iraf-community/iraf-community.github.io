<div>
So far that sounds normal:  The BITPIX keyword isn't retained however it is printed out when you run RFITS.  So, if may well be BITPIX=8 in the FITS file, but afterward the .imh image is a (unsigned) short with the scaling applied.  Having a BITPIX=8 and scaling in the FITS is allowed, it just means there will be 256 quantized levels in the pixel values.<br>
<br>
Are the resiults of an IMSTAT on the converted image what you expect?<br>
<br>
-Mike
