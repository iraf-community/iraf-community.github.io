<div>
Without seeing the data or your parameters it's hard to say more than an 'arithmetic exception' is something like a divide-by-zero, log() of a negative number, etc.  It is sometimes a coding error but in a heavily used command/task more often it is data or parameter related.  Try unlearning the task and resetting the params slowly to see whether it makes a difference.  You might also look at the pixel values with the 'z' key to see if they "should work".  Otherwise, please post a url to the image and your parameters (i.e. "cl&gt; dpar imexam").<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
