<div>
<br>
<br>
DS9 v7.1 and later are broken in various ways.  The fix for your problem is I think to do<br>
<br>
    setenv IMTDEV inet:5137<br>
<br>
to force use of the inet socket instead of the broken (in ds9) unix socket.  Do this in the window before you login to iraf/pyraf.<br>
<br>
</div>
{% endraw %}
