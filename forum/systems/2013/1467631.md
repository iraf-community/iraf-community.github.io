# msctpeak fits fail miserably

**Frank Winkler** wrote on Feb 25, 2013

{% raw %}
<div>
I use msctpeak often for tweaking the WCS on images--both using mosaics and for single images.  Usually this works just fine.  But occasionally I get images where the original WCS is pretty close to correct, yet when I mark and center some stars and do a fit, the residuals are enormous--hundreds of arcsec.   I've seen this behavior on images from different telescopes, often when the true residuals should be 1 arcsec or less (the circles marking the stars come up bang on the stars in the image).   <br>
<br>
(I just posted another unrelated message about msctpeak, the present post relates to a long-successful installation running IRAF 2.14 under Mac OS10.6)<br>
