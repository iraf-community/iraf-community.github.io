Hi Wei-Chun,<br>
	The asin/acos functions were never implemented in the original<br>
CL for some reason, however they ARE available in the Enhanced CL<br>
(ECL) available from the <a href="http://iraf.noao.edu">http://iraf.noao.edu</a> homepage.  The ECL is<br>
distributed as a self-installing script, to use it you would just<br>
type 'ecl' instead of 'cl' to begin.  Hope this helps.<br>
