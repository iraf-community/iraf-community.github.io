No matter what the scripting language, 3847 lines is actually quite  <br>

overflow, but it isn't surprising that you're challenging the size of  <br>

scripts/search.faq?pattern=dictionary&amp;type=and, although I wouldn't  <br>
typically suggest recompiling the CL.<br>
<br>
Second, I note that you modified the script yesterday.  Did it work  <br>
before you made the latest change?<br>
<br>
&gt; It crashes after having processed 24 or 49 spectra (I tried running<br>
&gt; it twice with a long list of input spectra to process, one at a time).<br>
<br>
Is it possible to simply run the spectra in two batches?<br>
<br>
You might see if the problem goes away under the new Enhanced CL:   <br>
<a href="http://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/web/new_stuff/ecl_beta.html">http://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/web/new_stuff/ecl_beta.html</a><br>
<br>
I hope some of these suggestions help.<br>

Hi Marianne,<br>
<br>
&gt; Also, in this day and age, a length of a spectrum of 4000 pixels  <br>
&gt; isn't unusual.<br>
<br>
Sorry - I think we're talking at cross purposes.  I understood you to  <br>
say that your script file - the program, not the spectra, that is -  <br>
was close to 4000 lines long.  As you say, a 4000 pixel spectrum is  <br>
nothing special.  IRAF tasks should be able to handle spectra many,  <br>
many thousands (if not millions) of pixels long.<br>
<br>
The dictionary full message has nothing to do with the size of the  <br>
spectra - or even the number of the spectra, rather with the size or  <br>
complexity of the script.  Typically this might involve calling some  <br>
set of tasks repeatedly within a loop and for whatever reason the  <br>
tasks keep getting added to the end of the dictionary rather than  <br>
replacing previous entries.<br>
<br>
&gt; Do you mean that I run the spectra in batches of ~40 spectra?<br>
<br>
That was the suggestion.<br>
<br>
&gt; Well, I already have 46000 spectra to process in batches of several  <br>
&gt; hundred spectra at a time, so....<br>
<br>
Are you familiar with "@-files" for specifying a list of images (or  <br>
spectra)?  If the current version of your script is found to work on  <br>
batches of a few dozen spectra, you could divide the 46000 spectra up  <br>
into separate @-files of a few dozen each.  Then populate a new  <br>
script with commands like:<br>
<br>
mytask @file1<br>
mytask @file2<br>
mytask @file3<br>
...<br>
<br>
and run the whole thing as one batch.  You might find that  <br>
interspersing the magic word "flpr" helps:<br>
<br>
mytask @file1<br>
flpr<br>
mytask @file2<br>
flpr<br>
...<br>
<br>
The bottom line is that I don't know why your script is now  <br>
overflowing the dictionary.  It sounds like it worked before your  <br>
most recent change to the logic of the script. (?)  I presume,  <br>
however, that you care more about processing the spectra instead of  <br>
debugging the script.  Did you happen to try the enhanced CL as  <br>
suggested in the previous message?<br>
