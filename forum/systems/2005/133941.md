On Wed, 26 Oct 2005, Buell Jannuzi wrote:<br>
<br>
&gt; Warning, IRAF question... when setting stdgraph to psdump,<br>
&gt; how can one adjust the density, or resolution, of the image that is saved?<br>
<br>
You can't do it from the command line or a ":.snap" other than to <br>
create a private graphcap file and your own device entry with the<br>
specified size/resolution.  For instance, to create your own graphcap<br>
file do:<br>
<br>
	cl&gt; copy dev$graphcap home$mygraphcap<br>
	cl&gt; reset graphcap = home$mygraphcap<br>
	cl&gt; gflush<br>
<br>
The last two lines you can put in your login(user).cl file, the gflush<br>
is needed to reinitialize the system.  Then to create your own device<br>
entry add something like:<br>
<br>
     mypsdump|		:XO#300:XW#1950:YO#1000:YW#1300:tc=g=psdump:<br>
<br>
The XO/YO (x-oh/y-oh, not x-zero/Y-zero) specify the offset of the <br>
plot on the page, the XW/YW and the size of the plot in pixels.  You<br>
can do the math to figure out how to make a particular size given a<br>
300 dpi printer.  Then just snap to device 'mypsdump', remember to<br>
'gflush' after the plot and after each change to the graphcap file.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike<br>
