Hi Virginie,<br>
<br>
In my experience the best color images come from working in Photoshop or GIMP since you have far greater control.  There is a detailed tutorial on this by Travis Rector (<a href="http://xxx.lanl.gov/abs/astro-ph/0412138)">http://xxx.lanl.gov/abs/astro-ph/0412138)</a> and by using his techniques, I was able to make much nicer color images that I'd been able to do before (using RGBSUN).<br>
<br>
I believe GIMP can open fits files directly and there is a free plugin (described in the Travis Rector tutorial) which opens fits files in photoshop and gives you great control over display parameters.<br>
<br>
Let us know if you get a nice color image out of any of this.<br>
<br>
Josh Walawender
