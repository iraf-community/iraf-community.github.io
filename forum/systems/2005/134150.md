	I briefly read your web page and while editing the various script<br>
files to look for an 'openbsd' architecure dir will mostly work, you<br>
should know that compiling binaries relies on an arch definition in the<br>
iraf#unix/os/irafpath.c file that will look specifically for one of the<br>
named architectures (e.g. 'redhat' or 'freebsd') and so compilation under<br>
you system may not work.<br>
	If the linux emulation is link-compatible then a better scheme <br>
may be to simply symlink the bin.openbsd dirs to bin.redhat in your <br>
script.  <br>
<br>
&gt; (b) OpenBSD was a supported architecture in cl.sh, irafuser.csh, and <br>

planning to deal with future IRAF support, and how the IRAF developers<br>
are planning to deal with NOAO's plans.  OpenBSD is a niche platform and<br>
so formal support won't likely be coming soon.<br>
