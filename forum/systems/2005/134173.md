The message means there is probably a permissions problem on the <br>
/home/iraf directory, e.g. it was created by root and isn't readable by<br>
anyone else.  To change the permissions use 'chmod', e.g.<br>
<br>
	chmod 755 /home/iraf<br>
<br>
will grant the proper permissions on the directory.  Do this as root if<br>
that's who owns the files, otherwise as the owner.  Note the <br>
subdirectories may be similarly affected, use the command <br>
<br>
	chmod -R g+r,o+r /home/iraf<br>
<br>
to recursively grant read permission to the directory tree.<br>
