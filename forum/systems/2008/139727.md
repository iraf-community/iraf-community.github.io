<div>
Be sure you are declaring the task to have parameters, i.e.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>task testm = /path/testm.cl

AND NOT

task $testm = /path/testm.cl</code></pre><br>
<br>
The first declaration works for me, the second prompts me for imagelist.  A '$' in the declaration means the task has no parameters, however any variable declared before the 'begin' statement are considered parameters.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
