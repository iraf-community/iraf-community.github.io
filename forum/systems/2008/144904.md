The app-defaults files are normally found using the XFILESEARCHPATH environment variable, e.g. in your .cshrc file<br>
<br>
<pre><code> 
setenv XFILESEARCHPATH &quot;:/usr/lib/X11/%t/%N%S:/usr/x11R6/%T/%N%S:/usr/local/lib/%T/%N%S&quot;</code></pre><br>
<br>
will do it for most systems.<br>
