XAPPHOT makes use of the imaging part of the widget in the GUI, most likely you're seeing the problem of using this on a 24-bit display (which is known to not work).  The only workaround for the moment is to use an 8-bit Pseudocolor display as you would for XImtool.  Work on the widget to fix this is underway now.<br>
