**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jan 28, 2008

<div>
Nick,<br>
<br>
The 'imdir' is only used for IMH format images (to store .pix files), for FITS files it isn't relevant but you do need to be in the same directory as the images to avoid specifying a path.  Try doing<br>
<br>
[code]<br>
cl&gt; cd /iraf/imdirs/Nick<br>
cl&gt; display image.fits 1<br>
<br>
or<br>
<br>
cl&gt; display imdir$image.fits 1<br>
[code]<br>
<br>
Also, your 'imdir' is required to have a trailing '/' character to be valid, and in your case you appear to be missing the leading '/' as well.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
