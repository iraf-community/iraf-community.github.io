PC-IRAF is known to work under Vista, but you'll need to have Cygwin and X11 installed first along with a C-shell (see cygwin.com).  The core IRAF distribution is the same for all platforms, you'll need to get the ib.cygw.x86 and nb.cygw.x86 binaries.<br>
