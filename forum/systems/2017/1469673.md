                I have no experience with Ureka; however: is the "libncurses5:i386" or the "lib32ncurses5" package installed?<br>
<br>
"/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libtinfo.so.5" is for 64 bit only, and if the IRAF version is 32-bit, it can't be used.<br>
