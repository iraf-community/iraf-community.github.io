You would simply specify the 'overwrite' attribute, i.e.<br>
<br>
   gm&gt; imcopy smngsdata_1 mngsdata_1[1,overwrite]<br>
<br>
See the FITS kernel user's guide at <a href="http://iraf.net/irafdocs/fits_userguide/">http://iraf.net/irafdocs/fits_userguide/</a> for more information.<br>
