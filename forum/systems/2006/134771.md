<div>
The Ctrl-N and Ctrl-P keys will move between the next/previous pages on the screen, otherwise the up/down arrows should be all you need to scroll.  Check also that your keyboard NumLock key isn't on and causing the numeric keypad to be used instead of the arrows.  The exact keys depend on how your 'editor' CL environment variable is set, i.e. either as 'vi' or 'emacs' which have slightly different sequences.  The often overlooked ESC-? prompt at the bottom right of the window will show you the commands used for your settings.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
