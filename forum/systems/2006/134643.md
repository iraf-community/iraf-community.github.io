Hi Naz,<br>
<br>
This has nothing to do with IRAF.<br>
<br>
However, my suggestion is to reconfigure and build your kernel with ntfs support.<br>
<br>
FYI, the kernel config option is "CONFIG_NTFS_FS".<br>
<br>
You should probably NOT set CONFIG_NTFS_RW, as writing to an NTFS drive with linux is still experimental.<br>
<br>
Good luck!<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
<br>
