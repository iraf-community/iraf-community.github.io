"An addition to the previous reply":<br>
<br>
If you want to have the global header (i.e. from in.fits[0]) appended to<br>
your extension header then add the 'inherit+'  parameter value:<br>
<br>
imcopy in.fits[1][inherit+]  out.fits<br>
<br>
out.fits will contain the extension header plus the header from in.fits[0] (Global header).<br>
