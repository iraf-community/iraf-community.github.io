On Wed, 28 Dec 2005, Fernando Camilo wrote:<br>
<br>
&gt; Could you please let me know whether there are any known issues for IRAF <br>
&gt; running under Linux Debian 3.1 (sarge)?  We are building a new Linux-based <br>
&gt; computing network here at the Columbia Astrophysics Lab and want to make <br>
&gt; sure that the key packages will work under our proposed operating system.<br>
<br>
	There are no particular issues assuming you use the "linux" <br>
architecture and our distribution.  The various debian packages *do*<br>
have known problems with compiling locally and/or running at all and<br>
appear to have since been abandoned by the package maintainer.  64-bit<br>
systems still aren't supported but other wise proceed with a normal<br>
installation using the LNUX architecture and you should be fine.<br>
