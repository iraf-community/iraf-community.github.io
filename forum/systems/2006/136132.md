**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Oct 29, 2006

{% raw %}
<div>
Compilation is not supported on 64-bit systems however the 32-bit binaries are known to work.   What this means is that you'll need to compile your programs on a 32-bit system and move them over when done.  Full support for 64-bit systems is on the list of projects but is a fairly big effort due to the amount of testing required even beyond the basic port.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
