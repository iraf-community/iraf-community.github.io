You're not doing anything wrong, this is why we have V2.13.  The GCC 4 compilers as well as some OS changes that perennially seem to break the &lt;varargs.h&gt; stuff in the system means that V2.12.2 won't build on newer machines and newer compilers.  This has been fixed in V2.13 of course.<br>
