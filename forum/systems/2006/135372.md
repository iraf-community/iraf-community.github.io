<div>
Hello Ross,<br>
<br>
the Setup.bash is not part of the iraf system, but comes from the scisoft installation, and is used by each user to set the right environment variables needed by scisoft applications (and then, by iraf).<br>
<br>
<blockquote><br>
barbara@ubuntu:/scisoft/bin$ . /scisoft/bin/Setup.bash <br>
bash: [: missing `]' <br>
bash: i686: command not found <br>
</blockquote><br>
<br>
this could be a syntax error in a "test operator" like:<br>
<br>
if [ -e some_file ]<br>
 then ...<br>
 else ...<br>
fi<br>
<br>
or other kind of tests (that is, something different inside square brackets). <br>
I remember there were some bugs in the scisoft scripts (at least in the OSX version...).<br>
Could you please please post your setup.bash script, or at least the section that causes the error?<br>
<br>
For the next problem: <br>
<blockquote><br>
The AS, IB, and NB distribution files are shown where they <br>
*** should be unpacked. Please verify the structure and the <br>
*** system architecture. <br>
</blockquote><br>
<br>
my guess is that, since the Setup.bash failed and did not set right value for IRAFARCH for example, the installation script could not figure out the right kind of system... <strong>maybe</strong> you can try with:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
./install -m linux
</code></pre><br>
<br>
to force the installer to use lnux binaries, but <strong>I'm not sure</strong> :shock:<br>
<br>
You should also try to contact Scisoft support or helpdesk, since, I guess, your problems are due to the failing Setup.bash script...<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
Emiliano.<br>
<br>
P.S. could you please post the output of command "set" (i.e. your environment variables) after the error in Setup.bash ?
