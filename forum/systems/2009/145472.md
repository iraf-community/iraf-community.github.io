<div>
If you're on Ubuntu, the binaries need to be in the package 'bin.linux' directory.  You can use Redhat binaries, just don't actually put them in a bin.redhat directory, they won't be found (that's what 'cannot open connected subprocess' is telling you).<br>
<br>
-Mike
