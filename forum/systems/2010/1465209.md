Just a word of warning for those installing FITSUTIL using both IRAF 2.15alpha and the FITSUTIL source as modified for the 2.15 release.  To get FITSUTIL to work properly, I had to change instances of "pkg/" to "src/" in the file "fitsutil.cl."<br>
<br>
(I am installing on a 64-bit Snow Leopard machine, but I think this point is irrelevant.)<br>
<br>
Regards,<br>
