<div>
You can use the binaries just posted to<br>
<br>
<a href="http://iraf.net/ftp/iraf/x11iraf/x11iraf-v1.5DEV-bin.redhat.tar.gz">http://iraf.net/ftp/iraf/x11iraf/x11iraf-v1.5DEV-bin.redhat.tar.gz</a><br>
<br>
These were built on a similar system and will look for the libncurses.so.5 on your system.  Alternatively you can log in as 'root' and create a link for the library, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code># cd /usr/lib
# ln -s libncurses.so.5 libncurses.so.4</code></pre><br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
