**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Nov 11, 2007

{% raw %}
<div>
Scisoft does weird things to bury the path.  If /iraf/iraf/unix/bin.suse doesn't work, log into the CL and issue the commands:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cl&gt; cd host$
cl&gt; path
</code></pre><br>
<br>
That will tell you the path to the iraf$unix directory, there should be a 'bin.suse' and 'bin.redhat' in there.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
