**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Mar 15, 2011

{% raw %}
<div>
I think what you're seeing is the effect of this change:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
lib/gio.h
sys/gio/cursor/gtr.h
    Increased the size of the WCS buffer.  The size was previously calculated
    as being 17 structure elements by assuming the SZ_INT was 2.  Increased
    to accomodate 64-bit sizes and will live with the wasted space.
</code></pre><br>
<br>
The specific change is to the GP_WCSPTR in gio.h and LEN_TRSTRUCT in gtr.h (diff the files for details).
</div>
{% endraw %}

**sontag** wrote on Mar 15, 2011

<div>
Thanks a lot.  That directed me to the change in gio.h where LEN_WCS was changed from 11 to 12.  In the end, that was what I need to work around in PyRAF.  The size of the WCS metacode burst is now 384 instead of 352.<br>
<br>
For any PyRAF users reading this post, the graphics changes to IRAF are now being correctly handled (at least for 32-bit) in the current version of PyRAF (i.e. the nightly tar-ball until v1.11 is out).<br>
<br>
Thanks again,<br>
Chris
