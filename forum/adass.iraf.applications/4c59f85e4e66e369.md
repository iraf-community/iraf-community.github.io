<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
You can use kill from standard UNIX command, after &quot;ps -aux&quot;, you can find
a proces that you want to cancel, and then put &quot;kill -9&quot;
followed by the number of the proces seen from &#x27;ps -aux&#x27;.
