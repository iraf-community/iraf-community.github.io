Hello Christophe,

    Mkmask is borrowed directly from the old dimsum package. Although I have
replaced a set of imreplace calls with a single imexpr call, I have not
changed the definition or use of flagval, at least not intentionally!  I think
the &quot;# Set flag ...&quot; comment in the mkmask script is misleading.  Flagval
is just a number outside the usual data range for the normalized noise image.

    I am more concerned about the floating point error out of imexpr or
possibly imarith ? I have had reports of floating point errors out of imexpr on
DecAplha but only in expressions involving exponentiation. This problem
disappeared with different (&quot;later&quot;) versions of the operating system. I have
also had reports of occasional &quot;high performance trap&quot; errors in imexpr, and
other tasks, again on a DecAlpha, but have not been able to duplicate them here.
Are you running xdimsum on a DecAlpha ? Is it possible your input images
contain funny for example very large numbers numbers.

of your input to mkmask and a listing of your mkmask parameters. You can
