# Gaussian convolving spectra with wavelength dependent width

**Frank Valdes** wrote on Aug 03, 1999

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
Does anyone knows a way to do the following in IRAF?


Q: I have used &quot;gauss&quot; to convolve spectra with a gaussian of a given width.
Is there a task that can convolve with a gaussian of wavelength-dependent
width? (to mimic a spectral resolution varying with lambda)...

A: I am not aware of any easy way to do what you want.  It could be done with
a clever script though great care would be needed to do this in a reasonably
efficient way.  Possibly the best suggestion would be to write your
own Fortran or C program and use either the IMFORT library (a library
for accessing IRAF imh images in a Fortran program) or WSPECTEXT and
RSPECTEXT to convert to and from an intermediate text file.
</pre>
