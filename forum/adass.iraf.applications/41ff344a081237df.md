## DEREDDEN - Removing reddening correction

**Frank Valdes** wrote on Dec 08, 1997

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">

Q:  Suppose I have &quot;deredden&quot;ed spectrum, and I wish to have un&quot;deredden&quot;ed
spectrum. (for some reason I&#x27;m under this situation, or I guess it happens
when one wish to reanalyze the spectrum)

How could I un&quot;derredden&quot; the spectrum?  Is it possible to do that? or
I need to do calculate that myself?


A:  It is not obvious but at the end of the help page and in one of the
examples for the task DEREDDEN it shows that you can use a negative
extinction coefficient to  &quot;redden&quot; a spectrum.  If you just want
to undo a previous operation then change the sign of the extinction
value you used.
</pre>
