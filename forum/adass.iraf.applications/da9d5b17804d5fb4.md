# Registering images with IMALIGN without losing pixels

**Christophe Jean** wrote on Dec 07, 2001

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
Dear all,

I would like to align a list of images to a reference image and obtain images of
a common size which would be big enough to include all the pixels. (I am not
sure I am very clear.)
Because IMSHIFT preserves the size of the input images, the output images are
truncated in the directions of the shifts.
Is there a way to tell IMSHIFT not to preserve the size of the input images and
to enlarge the output images as necessary?
It is certainly easy but I didn&#x27;t find the answer in the newsgroup nor in the
FAQ.

Many thanks in advance.

Best regards,

Christophe
</pre>

---

**Gulab Chand Dewangan** wrote on Dec 07, 2001

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
Dear  Christophe,
     You can use the tasks &quot;rheader&quot; and then &quot;reflresamp&quot; available
within STSDAS for your purpose. This will work even if the pixel sizes
and orientations are different provided the world coordinates have been
updated in the image headers.

Gulab
