## IMEDIT for spectra

**Frank Valdes** wrote on Jun 19, 1998

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">

Q:  Is there any way to do the equivalent of &quot;imedit&quot; on a 1-D spectrum
in a .ms format?? We&#x27;ve been looking and looking and haven&#x27;t been able
to come up with anything.


A:  I think what you want is SPLOT.  It has &#x27;x&#x27; for editing pixel values
and &#x27;i&#x27; to write out the modified spectrum.  There is also the
xydraw option (set in the parameters or with :xydraw [yes|no]) which
controls whether to interpolate from the indicated endpoints selected
with the x position of the cursor or actually draw a line between the
x,y cursor positions.  The works on ms format and can write out spectra
to either separate images, replace in the input image, or write to
a new multispec image.  Is this what you mean by equivalent?
</pre>
