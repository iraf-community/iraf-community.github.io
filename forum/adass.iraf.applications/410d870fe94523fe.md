There are many steps in doing astrometry and there are a variety of 
tools in IRAF to do these steps.  There are tasks for centroiding stars 
and fitting coordinate functions.  The main tools to mention are CCMAP, 
FINDER, MSCTPEAK.  There is no single package though the IMCOORDS 
package would be the closest.  There is also no cookbook, which I think 
is one of the things you are hoping for. The only other advice I can 
give you is to look at

http://iraf.noao.edu/projects/ccdmosaic/astrometry/astrom.html
