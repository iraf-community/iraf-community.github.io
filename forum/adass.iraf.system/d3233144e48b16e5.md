CDi_j is the partial derivative of the ith &quot;world&quot; coordinate (identified
by CTYPEi) with respect to the jth pixel axis.

So (1) is closer, but you need an additional conversion from angles in
the tangent plane to RA &amp; Dec.  Roughly speaking, you need to divide
CD1_1*(x-CRPIX1) + CD1_2*(y-CRPIX2) by cos (Dec) before adding CRVAL1.
