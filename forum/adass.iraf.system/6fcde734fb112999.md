## XImtool cursor colors

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Feb 05, 1998

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">

&gt;         I have a terribly minor question:  is there control over the color
&gt; of the ximtool cursor (and the ximtool graphics input cursor) color?  I
&gt; have been hunting around through the faq and man pages and cannot find how
&gt; to manipulate it -- without changing the color of all my foreground text
&gt; in all windows, that is (*Foreground does that).  Why is this important at
&gt; all?  Well, in reverse video when stars are dark, the black cross in
&gt; ximtool is hard to see against the stellar disk...

        Have a look at the following resources:

           idleCursorBgColor   busyCursorBgColor   ginmodeCursorBgColor
           idleCursorFgColor   busyCursorFgColor   ginmodeCursorFgColor

These will change the various cursor colors.  You can also use the Shift-MB2
in the main display window to bring up a fullscreen crosshair cursor, it&#x27;s
not only a better way to center on small features but should work just as
well in reverse video.

Cheers,
-Mike
</pre>
