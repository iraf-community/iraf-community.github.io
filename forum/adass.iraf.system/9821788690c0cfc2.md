Hello Sergei,

The formula is correct appart from a multiplication by the wavelength
per pixel to convert the integration over pixels to an integration over
wavelength and to give the equivalent width the units of wavelength.

Yes the answer depends on the number of pixels used.  But in an ideal
isolated line profile the line intensities I(i) converge on the continuum
C(i) as you move away from the line center.  Thus the quantity being
summed becomes zero and so the equivalent width converges to a value
which is independent of the region being summed.  In practice in SPLOT
you mark the region to be summed to be points where the profile reaches
the continuum.
