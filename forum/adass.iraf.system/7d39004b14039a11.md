## HP offers free Fortran 90 upgrade

**Stephen Walton** wrote on Aug 15, 2000

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
This is, I think, relevant to those of you running IRAF on HP-UX.  HP
announced that they are discontinuing Fortran 77 in favor of their
Fortran 90 product.  They are offering a free upgrade to Fortran 90 for
current Fortran 77 owners through the end of this month (August 31,
2000).  See the Web page at 

http://devresource.hp.com/devresource/Topics/Porting/Port.html

for more information.  In particular, HP Fortran 77 will not support
64-bit program.
