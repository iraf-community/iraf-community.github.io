# XGterm and RedHat 6.0

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Sep 28, 1999

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
&gt; When I run xgterm under RHUX6 and I use the command &quot;less,&quot; the prompts
&gt; and ascii text in the window reverse video. have you seen this? Does not
&gt; happen with xterm or xgterm under RH5.2.

	You&#x27;ll need to reset your TERM environment variable to be
&#x27;xterm-color&#x27; instead of &#x27;xterm&#x27;.  On RH 6.0 the terminfo
&#x27;exit_standout_mode&#x27; (rmso) capability is set to &lt;esc&gt;[27M instead of
&lt;esc&gt;[m as it is for xterm-color (and xterm-old and probably others I
didn&#x27;t check).  I don&#x27;t know the reason for the change, but this is the
cause of the problem.

-Mike
</pre>
