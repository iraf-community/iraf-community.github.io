<div>
The 'command not found' messages mean your path environment variable doesn't include the location of either the 'bind' or 'xgterm' commands.  The 'bind' is probably being used to define the backspace key and is a bash built-in command, however xgterm is a standalone binary that was probably put into /usr/local/bin or some such place.  My guess is that when the machine was moved your account login directory may have somehow changed, and so the .login or .bashrc or .cshrc files aren't being found properly.<br>
