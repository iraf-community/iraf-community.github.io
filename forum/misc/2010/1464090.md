<div>
Sky brightness may also change due to weather conditions and/or light pollution.<br>
<br>
High cirrus clouds that might be invisible to the eye can still reflect significant enough amounts of light to vary the sky background. If possible - check weather data for the specific nights in question - barometric pressure as well as dew point temperature - that will tell you the amount of moisture in the atmosphere.<br>
