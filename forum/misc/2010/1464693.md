I am not a photometry expert but what you describe is reasonable.  My only comment is that since you have a sparse field and a relatively bright target source a large aperture should get photometry to the same level of accuracy.  As I understand your concern, the comparison sources produce larger scatter with aperture photometry.  Of course this could be because they are faint but it could also be that you are not using a large enough aperture to avoid seeing effects.<br>
<br>
I hope this is of some help.<br>
