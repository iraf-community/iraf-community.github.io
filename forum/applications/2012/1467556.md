Hi charly,<br>
<br>
Unfortunately, the tasks in the Gemini IRAF package do not allow you to selectively reduce specific extensions in the MEF files. I'm afraid you will have to re-reduce your data from the gswavelength step. It is recommended to run the gswavelength task interactively to confirm that the wavelength solution is good for each extension.<br>
