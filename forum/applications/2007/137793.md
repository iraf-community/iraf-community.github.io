**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jul 17, 2007

<div>
Depends on what's in the catalogs exactly, but have a look at the IMMATCH and IMCOORDS packages, specifically the XYXYMATCH task.  These don't work on FITS tables directly, however you can use the TABLES package to dump these to a text file and/or extract the columns you need.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
