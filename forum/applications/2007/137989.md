<div>
The XTOOLS are a library of SPP procedures, not actual tasks.<br>
<br>
To sum the pixels between two columns you can use something like the BLKAVG with option=sum, or just IMSTAT and then multiply the NPIX and MEAN values to get the sum.  Another idea might be:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; listpix img[320:330,123] | fields STDIN 2 | average</code></pre><br>
<br>
The 'sum' parameter in AVERAGE will be the sum of cols 320-330 on row 123.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
