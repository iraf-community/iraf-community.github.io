<br>
If I understand your question:  The sigma parameter must be in units of pixels, so if you know only the velocity you'll need to convert that to pixels based on the resolution of the detector.  If you're trying to do something like rotational broadening of spectra, do a forum search from the banner bar for 'rotbroad' and you'll find a script that can help.  (Note you may need to modify this a bit to get it working).  Otherwise, what kind of instrument is this?  An image or spectrum?  Why are you doing the convolution at all?<br>
