Dear Azamat,<br>
<br>
I think you are asking about "deconvolving" merged spectra?  There is nothing in the NOAO spectral packages that does this.  The option that is sometimes recommended if you are dealing with two close spectra is to use a one-sided aperture.  That means, for example, you define an aperture that extends from the center of the profile to wing on the side away from the other spectrum.  This will be a little noiser but it will be less affected by the other spectrum.  The relation between the total flux and the partial flux is an "aperture correction"; that is some constant number.<br>
<br>
I hope this helps.<br>
<br>
