Actually mscgetcatalog uses astcat.agetcat to access gsc2@stsci.edu.  What agetcat does is open a connection to stsci and query for data.  It appears that site is no longer available.  You could use gsc1@cadc.  Other than that you will need to get the data interactively from some website.<br>
