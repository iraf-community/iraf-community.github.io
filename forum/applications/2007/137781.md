<div>
Hi,<br>
<br>
The approach you should take is to edit the apertures using the core tracing as the reference.  In this mode you can shift the center by some amount or set the lower and upper edges (:lower, :upper or 'l' and 'u' keys (I think)) to something else.  Then extract while turning the tracing off.  For example is the aperture position and edges at the reference line/colum are something like<br>
<br>
256 324.3 -5 5<br>
<br>
You would change it to something like<br>
<br>
256 324.3 -35 -25 <br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
