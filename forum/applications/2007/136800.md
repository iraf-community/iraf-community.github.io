Hi Roy,<br>
<br>
IRAF is still limited to 32-bits and a floating-point image 75K on a side is<br>
something like 20Gb (well beyond the 2Gb 32-bit file size limit).  You can try combining small (&lt;2Gb chunks) but the final mosaic would have to be done outside of IRAF.  For example, I'd work in "strips" the full width of your final field, then somehow concatenate the result.<br>
<br>
-Mike
