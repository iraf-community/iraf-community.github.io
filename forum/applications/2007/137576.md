{% raw %}
<div>
Hi, Mike et al....<br>
<br>
I'm having a very peculiar problem with some IMACS spectral data from Magellan data.  doslit (in the ctio package) refuses to assign comps appropriately, and I eventually traced the problem to the fact that the headers contain "UT-TIME" as opposed to (say) just "UT" as the time stamp.  I attempted the following:<br>
<br>
hedit ccd*.fits "UT" "(UT-TIME)" up+ ver- show+ add+<br>
<br>
Now, what this SHOULD have done (in my humble opinon) was add a key word<br>
"UT" and assign the value from UT-TIME to it.  But, instead I get the error message:<br>
ERROR: Image header parameter not found (UT)<br>
After I've added the UT word to the header with a bogus values, and rerun the<br>
command (without the "add+") I get:<br>
ERROR: Image header parameter not found (TIME)<br>
<br>
So, I've come to the conclusion (rightly or wrongly) that the "-" is badly<br>
confusing things.<br>
<br>
Nevertheless I can do a <br>
hselect ccd*.fits UT-TIME yes<br>
and see the correct values.<br>
<br>
Suggestions?  I'm running 2.12.2 on a red hat 9 machine, if it makes any difference.  I've temporarily gotten around this by instead telling doslit to do<br>
a "match" rather than "interp" for the comps, and I'm specifying them one by one, but the real question is why isn't hedit ccd*.fits UT "(UT-TIME)" <br>
not working.<br>
<br>
I'll include a sample header.<br>
<br>
THANKS!<br>
---phil<br>

<div>
Hi Phil,<br>
<br>
I hate to say it, but see the help page:  The '@' symbol needs to be used to dereference params with a hyphen, otherwise it is interpreted as e.g. "UT minus TIME".  So, try<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; hedit ccd*.fits UT &#x27;(@&quot;UT-TIME&quot;)&#x27; up+ ver- show+ add+</code></pre><br>
<br>
This will update the image with a UT keyword that is the value stored in the UT-TIME keyword.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
