**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Dec 29, 2005

{% raw %}
<div>
'Parameter not found' messages are usually fixed with an UNLEARN of<br>
the task to reset the parameters.  Params are sometimes added/deleted<br>
between releases, but you uparm$ directory keeps track of the old settings<br>
until you unlearn them or reinitialize with a MKIRAF.  More obscure reasons<br>
for the error have to do with installation errors, write back if the above<br>
doesn't fix it first.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
