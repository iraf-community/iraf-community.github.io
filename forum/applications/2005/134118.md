Dear Doug,<br>
     I was forwarded your request via IRAF help and STScI help. I wrote the <br>
imaging polarimetry tasks in the stecf.impol package. <br>
<br>
  I looked at your images. They had some floating overflow values in them <br>
(- and + 3.38E38) which I am sure caused the automatic scaling in polimplot<br>
to fail. I replaced these values and looked again. The  StokesP.fits image <br>
does not seem to have sensible values - there are many negative values which <br>
doesnt make sense for polarization (it is supoposed to be in %, always <br>
positive). The StokesA.fits has a centrosymmetric pattern of position angles <br>
(it looks like a model to me). However the values go from -1.57 to +1.57 <br>
(neglecting a band of funnies across the middle of the image). polimplot <br>
expects position angles in degrees. I multipled the PA image by 57.296 <br>
(after replacing the large positive and negative values) and when I <br>
substituted<br>
a flat polarization map (30%) got a centro symmetric pattern of vectors when <br>
plotting so:<br>
<br>
polimplot inpol="rubpol2.fits" inpopa="rubpa2.fits" poi_err=no inpoer="" <br>
inpaer="" \<br>
xybin=1 poscal=0.80 polow=1.0 pohigh=100.0 pacorr=0.0 xysam=9 polab=no <br>
title="" xlabel="" \<br>
ylabel="" device="stdgraph" left=0.3402 right=0.6598 bottom=0.3402 top=0.6598 <br>
erase=yes<br>
<br>

There is a directory of demos which can be downloaded. <br>
