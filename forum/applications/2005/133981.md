The z1,z2 values are the intensity stretch parameters.  There is no  <br>
specific criteria for choosing the values.  The same things apply to  <br>
simple grey scale display that if one makes the faintest things  <br>
visible you may saturate other things.  For getting a good color  <br>
composite image it is largely a matter of trial-and-error to get the  <br>
result you want.  If you are asking for a way to get values that best  <br>
display the background you could use the display task with  <br>
zscale=yes.  This uses an algorithm that does fairly well for a broad  <br>
range of data.  You could take the values printed on the terminal and  <br>
move them into rgbsun and that might be helpful.<br>
<br>
I would also call your attention to the "export" task in the dataio  <br>
package.  This is a more modern task though with additional  <br>
complexity.  It implements the zscale algorithm as part of its  <br>
intensity stretching calculation.<br>
<br>
I hope this helps a little.<br>
