Hello Anupama,<br>
<br>
Thank you for including the data.  I do not believe there were any  <br>
significant changes between the two versions of IRAF for the aperture  <br>
extraction.  Unless you used exactly the same aperture definitions,  <br>
that is the database/ap* files, there could be other things going  <br>
on.  There are also parameters which are not recorded in the headers  <br>
which might differ.<br>
<br>
Since the sky is such a strong contaminant I might suggest that you  <br>
extract both an object and a sky aperture.  In other words, don't use  <br>
the sky subtraction features in APEXTRACT but instead extract a  <br>
spectrum centered on the object and one that is offset to sky.  Then  <br>
use the SKYTWEAK or TELLURIC tasks.  These are designed to  <br>
empirically adjust to give the best removal including any dispersion  <br>
axis shifts and intensity variations with position.<br>
<br>
I hope this helps.  Without having the exact set of parameters and  <br>
aperture definitions I can't comment further on the difference  <br>
between the two versions.<br>
