	Have a look at the BLKAVG task, it'll block average the entire<br>
image and output a new 2D image.  If you need to offset this by some <br>
amount you can use an image section as the input, if you're grabbing <br>
"random" boxes from various places around the image you can output all<br>
the numbers to a list and then in your script call something like <br>
RTEXTIMAGE to make an image out of it.  Hope this helps.<br>
