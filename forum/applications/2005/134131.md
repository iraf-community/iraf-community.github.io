That will give Cristina an answer, but it will be wrong.<br>
IRAF will fail to read the OBJCTRA and OBJCTDEC in that format.<br>
<br>
OBJCTRA = '21 36 54' /          Nominal Right Ascension of center of image <br>
OBJCTDEC= '+12 47 05' /         Nominal Declination of center of image <br>
<br>
IRAF expects this:<br>
OBJCTRA = '21:36 :54' /          Nominal Right Ascension of center of image <br>
OBJCTDEC= '+12:47:05' /         Nominal Declination of center of image <br>
<br>
And will interpret Cristina's file as this:<br>
OBJCTRA = '21:00:00' /          Nominal Right Ascension of center of image <br>
OBJCTDEC= '+12:00:00' /         Nominal Declination of center of image <br>
<br>
It appears that both formats are being used by the community.  How can we change IRAF to either:<br>
   A)  Read both formats properly without user intervention.<br>
   B)  Report an error when it cannot interpret the format correctly.<br>
<br>
Thanks!<br>
Paul
