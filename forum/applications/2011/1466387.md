A segmentation violation is usually a coding problem but can sometime be triggered by the input.   The TABLES binaries are unmodified from what is distributed by STScI and I'm not able to reproduce the problem using simple input, so your best bet may be to ask the STScI helpdesk (help@stsci.edu) to see if they are aware of any issues.  They will probably need to see your parameter settings (e.g. "cl&gt; dpar igi") and input igi file in order to debug this.
