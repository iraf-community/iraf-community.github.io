<div>
I tend just to do:<br>
<br>
imhead @files l+ | grep KEYWORD &gt; file.txt<br>
<br>
and either use AWK/SED to clean it up or do it in vi...<br>
<br>
ie:<br>
sed 's/KEYWORD=//' file.txt &gt; file2.txt<br>
<br>
If I want multiple ones I do a file for each and then joinlines them together...<br>
<br>
-M
