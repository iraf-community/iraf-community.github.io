**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jan 21, 2011

{% raw %}
<div>
You're right that geotran only preserves the flux of the total image (output pixels are multiplied by the Jacobian of the transform) and not individual objects (which would require modelling of the PSF and background).  Beyond that I can't really say what this does to the accuracy of the flux of an individual object, I believe though that the flux preservation accuracy was checked long ago.<br>
<br>
If somebody has looked into this more closely I hope they'll post another reply.
</div>
