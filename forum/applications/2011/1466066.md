<div>
I think your problem is with your coordinate file.  The third column should be 101 (which means frame=1 and tile=01) and not "logical".  But even easier, just use the x and y alone.  The purpose of the defkey is just so this will work with a simple list of pixel coordinates.  Another comment I would make is that it can sometimes cause problems to begin image names with a number because the CL or the task may try and interpret it as a number (in particular something like 35x might be turned into a hex number).  In this case it does not matter but as a general rule I recommend not doing it.<br>
