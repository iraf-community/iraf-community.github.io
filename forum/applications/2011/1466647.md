<div>
The 'gibberish' is the graphics metacode, and it means you're not using a graphics terminal (e.g. specifically an 'xterm' since that is what you appear to have configured ('xgterm' gibberish is different).  A gnome-terminal, rxvt, Terminal.app, iTerm, konsole, etcc are not graphics  terminals.
