# Subtraction of stars with saturated pixels.

**Douglas W. Shields** wrote on Mar 16, 2011

{% raw %}
<div>
I am attempting to remove stars from an image using the DAOPHOT package.  Specifically, I have stars with saturated pixels overlaying the image of a galaxy.  I want the galaxy to remain but the stars subtracted out.<br>
<br>
My analysis procedure includes the following:<br>
<br>
1. DAOFIND<br>
2. PHOT<br>
3. PSTSELECT<br>
4. PSF<br>
5. ALLSTAR (or, alternatively, PEAK and then NSUB)<br>
<br>
I have toggled the daopars:saturat parameter to "yes", but with no effect.  The saturated pixels are not affected by the subtraction at all.<br>
<br>
I also tried increasing datapars:datamax parameter to a value higher than the pixel saturation value.  As expected, this did cause parts of the star to be subtracted out, but the residual is still considerable.<br>
<br>
Any thoughts?<br>
<br>
Thank you,<br>

<div>
I don't think there is a specific way to do this that works or is part of an IRAF package.  The only suggestion, provided you are not having to do a very large amount of data, is to look at the IMEDIT task.  However that only allows you to cosmetically replace data, usually from a nearby region.<br>
<br>
Note that technically you cannot recover any real science flux under the staturated part of the star since the observed counts are no longer linear when saturated.<br>
