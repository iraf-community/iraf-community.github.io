Some of what you want can be done with the GEMINI.GEMTOOLS package, e.g. see the GEMARITH task.  Otherwise, the rule is that you need to specify the image extension to e.g. implot the image data.  For example,<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; implot foo.fits[SCI]</code></pre><br>
<br>
Some of the Gemini folks follow the forum and you may get a reply from them as well.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
