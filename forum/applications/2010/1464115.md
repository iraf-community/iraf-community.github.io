**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 27, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
Let me repeat:  DO NOT use 256 color mode!!  It will not work with the X11IRAF v2.0 version of ximtool you should be using.  Reset the X11 preferences to either use 'millions' of colors or the 'From Display' option, and use X11IRAF v2.0 described earlier.<br>
<br>
As for the FITS file, it looks just fine and works well for me (nice globular cluster, floating point values of -902.6 to 62090).  I can't explain the bus error, what version of IRAF are you using (i.e. "cl&gt; =version")?  Does IMSTAT or IMHEAD work on the image or also fail with a bus error?
</div>
{% endraw %}
