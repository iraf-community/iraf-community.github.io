<div>
The dimension mismatch error usually comes about by copying out a section of the image (e.g. taking a 2-D slice of a 3-D image but the WCSDIM keyword still says '3'), or mismatched WCS systems (e.g. an equatorial WCS for spectral data).  The WCSRESET should have worked but keep in mind if the WCS is inherited you may need to execute it on the primary header and/or the extension in question.<br>
<br>
Otherwise, you'll need to post the image header (primary and extn) so we can see what's going on.
