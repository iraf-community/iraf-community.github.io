# problem getting implot to accept multiple filenames

**scarcity** wrote on Feb 03, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
Hi,<br>
<br>
I have a new install of IRAF 2.12 on OpenSUSE 11.2. Everything seems to be working fine, except that I can't get implot to accept multiple files.<br>
<br>
e.g.<br>
ecl &gt; implot file1.fit<br>
works fine, as expected.<br>
<br>
ecl &gt; implot @filelist<br>
ERROR: Cannot open image (@filelist)<br>
<br>
ecl &gt; implot file1.fit,file2.fit<br>
ERROR: Cannot open image (file1.fit,file2.fit)<br>
<br>
Other tasks are accepting multiple files and lists, e.g <br>
ecl&gt; imstat @filelist<br>
#               IMAGE      NPIX      MEAN      MODE    STDDEV       MIN       MAX<br>
            file1.fit   8388608     2581.     2411.     2996.   -10624.   299635.<br>
            file2.fit   8388608     2621.     2078.     4322.   -11600.   268121.<br>
<br>
ecl&gt; imstat file1.fit,file2.fit<br>
#               IMAGE      NPIX      MEAN      MODE    STDDEV       MIN       MAX<br>
            file1.fit   8388608     2581.     2411.     2996.   -10624.   299635.<br>
            file2.fit   8388608     2621.     2078.     4322.   -11600.   268121.<br>
<br>
Any idea what's gone wrong here?
</div>
{% endraw %}

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Feb 03, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
The ability to use lists in IMPLOT wasn't added until the V2.12.2 release, you need to upgrade your IRAF (you should for other reasons) or else just use on image at a time.<br>
<br>
-Mike
