<div>
You really don't want to be using USNO-A2.0 anymore anyway.... ;)<br>
<br>
1)  Find out what catalogs your IRAF already knows about by starting IRAF,<br>
      getting in mscred and then typing:<br>
<br>
      aclist * verbose-<br>
<br>
2)  Pick one of them.  Might I suggest usnob1@noao  (Of course, I am just<br>
      kind of hoping mscgetcatalog gets sufficient epoch information that <br>
      proper motions can be applied or that at the very least high proper motion<br>
      stars are removed from the query)....<br>
<br>
3)  epar mscgetcatalog<br>
<br>
4)  change catalog to your selection (e.g. usnob1@noao)<br>
<br>
5)  Use mscgetcatalog.  That was enough to get it working for me.<br>
<br>
Jeff Larsen
</div>
