<div>
Hello Christian,<br>
<br>
Since it was some time since I had studied the IRAF WCS capabilities I did not remember.  IRAF does not currently support the CDELT/PC formulation.  It supports the CD formulation.  For various reasons the WCS standard ended up with both allowed.  IRAF does recognized CDELTn only based on an earlier FITS convention where there is no rotation was allowed.<br>
<br>
So to use your data with IRAF you will need to convert your header to IRAF format.  A general way to do this is:<br>
<br>
<blockquote>hedit &lt;images&gt; cd1_1 "(cdelt1*pc1_1)" add+<br>
hedit &lt;images&gt; cd1_2 "(cdelt1*pc1_2)" add+<br>
hedit &lt;images&gt; cd2_1 "(cdelt2*pc2_1)" add+<br>
hedit &lt;images&gt; cd2_2 "(cdelt2*pc2_2)" add+<br>
</blockquote><br>
<br>
Frank
