<div>
Courtesy of Matt McMaster,<br>
Senior Research and Instrument Analyst - ACS/WFPC2 Team<br>
<br>
"When you requested the data from the archive, did the datafiles have the<br>
form: u9q7320?m_c0m.fits?  Did you use strfit to convert them to c0h/c0d files?<br>
 If so, you shouldn't have.  The c0m files you get from MAST are in Multi<br>
Extension Fits (MEF) format and can be used with Multidrizzle as is.  If a<br>
piece of documentation or information somewhere said that you should use<br>
c0h/c0d files, please disregard it and see below."<br>
<br>
This solved the problem.
