<br>
The whole point of external packages is that they have a development schedule and release cycle all their own (after all, GEMINI was updated in the middle of the v2.14.1 lifecycle as well).  While we try to keep dependencies in mind (e.g. preserving the old nhedit task by moving it to GMISC), packages will evolve as needed.  If the GEMINI package has specific version requirements, those downloads should be maintained along with the GEMINI package on a Gemini site so users aren't confused by directions to get a package from a second source.  Better yet is to include required code into the package directly and avoid dependencies where possible.<br>
<br>
I'll tell you now that when I get around to revising the downloads area of iraf.net in the coming weeks, it will be all the new versions.  Obsoleted packages won't be available here but will still be available from the ftp archive on iraf.noao.edu.  Adjust your page links accordingly when the time comes.
</div>
