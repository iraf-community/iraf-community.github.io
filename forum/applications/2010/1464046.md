**Francisco Valdes** wrote on Apr 09, 2010

{% raw %}
<div>
<pre><code>
for (i=1; i&lt;=10; i+=1) {
    printf (&quot;%s[*,%d]\n&quot;, &quot;20070430-GJ1156free&quot;, i) | scan (s1)
    imstat (s1)
}
</code></pre><br>
<br>
The use of parenthesis and quotes is important in "programming" mode to distinguish variables (like i and s1) from fixed strings like the image name.  Clearly there are many variations you can use including:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
imstat (s1, &gt; &quot;output.dat&quot;)
</code></pre><br>
<br>
To capture the output.  Also if your image is 3D (because of extra bands) then you would use "%s[*,%d,1]".<br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
