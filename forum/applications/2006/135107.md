**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on May 31, 2006

{% raw %}
<div>
If what you want is to simply clip all pixel values above 2 to the value 2, then look at IMREPLACE, e.g.<br>
<br>
cl&gt; imreplace spec* 2 lower=2.0<br>
<br>
should do what you want.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
