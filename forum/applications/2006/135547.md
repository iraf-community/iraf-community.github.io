Hopefully you'll get advice about which parameters to tweak to avoid this from somebody who knows more about the task, but a 'singlular' solution means a unique solution couldn't be found when solving the matrix, a 'merged' error means that a "fainter star &lt; 0.37 * fwhmpsf from a brighter star in the group".<br>
