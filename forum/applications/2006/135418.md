First my apologies for the lack of documentation.<br>
<br>
Mike is correct about the values.  My only additional comment, which you may already understand, is that MSCCMATCH only applies a global linear correction to the existing geometry information derived from an astrometric field previously.  This existing information includes distortions and the detailed placement and orientations of the CCDs.  It is assumed that this has not changed from the calibration to your observations.  The global linear accounts for the telescope pointing, rotations when mounting the camera, and the effects of refraction.<br>
