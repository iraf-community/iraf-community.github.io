<div>
If I understand you correctly you want to rescale the 512*512 image to the pixelscale of the 3000*3000 image? So what goes wrong is that your small image conserve its pixelscale and only tries to shift the smaller image into the best-fit position inside the larger image? When you run geomap, how good is the fit?<br>
<br>
If you want geotran to change the pixelscale of your image,  you must have a geomap database where the fitting has been made with free scaling of the image (i.e. with fitgeometry set to rscale, rxyscale or general). Also in the geotran parameters the xmag and ymag params should be "INDEF".<br>
<br>
Hope this helps, sorry if I'm stating something obvious <br>
<br>
//Jens
