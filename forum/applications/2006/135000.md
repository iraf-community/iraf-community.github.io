<div>
Hi Phil,<br>
<br>
The '&gt;&gt;G' is a CL level redirection, try something like<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; igi &lt; file1.com device=&quot;stdgraph&quot; append+ &gt;&gt;G my.metacode</code></pre><br>
<br>
similar to the way you would redirect/append text output onto a file.  Can't comment on the ST help pages, not listing params at the top is their standard format but <br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; help igi secton=parameters</code></pre><br>
<br>
should always bring you right to it.  Hope this helps.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}
