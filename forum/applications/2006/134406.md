<div>
Looks like the kind of gibberish you get when either the XGterm/Xterm settings are wrong (e.g. using an XGterm window but told IRAF it was an Xterm), or you're using something that doesn't support graphics at all like a gnome-terminal or rxvt.  Double check your terminal and settings, a new MKIRAF or an "cl&gt; stty xgterm" or "cl&gt; stty xterm" as appropriate should help.<br>
