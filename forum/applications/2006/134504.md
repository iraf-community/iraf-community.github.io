We occasionally have seen problems when parameters in tables package tasks have changed.  I don't think this is the problem here, although you may want to think about updating various of your software just from best practices.<br>
<br>
Our best guess is the version of the GSC catalog you are attempting to use.  Finder only supports catalog input in the original format of the GSC.  Look at the mkgsctab task, for instance.  You may use any catalog you want as long as the entries are converted to the original GSC format.  I suspect the more recent GSC-ACT catalog is simply not recognized in its native format.  Take a look at mkgsctab and see if it does what you want.<br>
