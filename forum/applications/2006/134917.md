<div>
My experience with Mosaic (both the northern and southern) is that one <br>
expects that the RMS of a fit is about 0.2-0.3".  If it is significantly larger<br>
than this, such as 1", chances are quite good that you are fitting noise or<br>
confused identifications. <br>
<br>
If it helps any, have a look at how we reduced the Local Group Galaxies<br>
Survey Data: <br>
<a href="http://www.lowell.edu/users/massey/lgsurvey/splog2.html">http://www.lowell.edu/users/massey/lgsurvey/splog2.html</a><br>
specifically section 5.<br>
<br>
Hope this helps.<br>
<br>
---phil massey
