# Building gemini pkg & deps with Ubuntu 6 + 2.13beta2

**James Turner** wrote on Nov 27, 2006

{% raw %}
<div>
Following some initial difficulties under 2.12.2a, I thought I'd mention that I was able to compile the Gemini IRAF package and all of its dependencies under IRAF 2.13 beta 2 on Ubuntu 6.06 with no problems (note that gemini currently still recommends using 2.12.2a, but I have switched due to an SPP compilation problem that I had under Ubuntu).<br>
<br>
This packages involved are the following:<br>
<ul>tables 3.6   (although gemini 1.9 nominally uses 3.5)<br>
stsdas 3.6   (although gemini 1.9 nominally uses 3.5)<br>
fitsutil<br>
nmisc<br>
gmisc<br>
color<br>
gemini 1.9</ul><br>
For IRAF itself, I used the "redhat" binaries since "linux" binaries haven't been distributed yet, unpacking them in iraf/iraf/bin.linux and iraf/iraf/noao.bin.linux and copying the contents of iraf/iraf/unix/bin.redhat to iraf/iraf/unix/bin.linux. Note that you <strong>must</strong> keep the original copies of the HSI binaries in iraf/iraf/unix/bin.redhat as well, otherwise you won't be able to compile stuff because xc is hard-wired to look there when it is built (thanks, Mike, for explaining that). You also need to get the updated fcgetcoords.x from <a href="http://www.gemini.edu/sciops/data/dataSoftware.html">http://www.gemini.edu/sciops/data/dataSoftware.html</a>) and recompile the longslit package, so that some of the gemini tasks work properly (should be unnecessary in the final 2.13).<br>
<br>
To get the ecl working with Ubuntu, see the thread at <a href="http://iraf.net/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=84851">http://iraf.net/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=84851</a>.<br>
<br>
