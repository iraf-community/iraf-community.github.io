<div>
Hi Pedro,<br>
<br>
I'll leave it to Frank to comment on a possible FIXPIX/CCDMASK bug, I'm sure it would help him if you could post a sample mask (or URL to one).  As for some of your other questions:<br>
<br>
<blockquote>I found a strange thing, which can be completely unrelated and only reported here due to my ignorance, but here it goes. If I dispay the mask in a ds9 window with the command<br>
<br>
display mask.pl 1 zscale- z1=0. z2=2.0<br>
<br>
and use the pixel table, for instance, I find that the pixels do not have the value of 1 and 2 but 0.984925 and 1.96985... </blockquote><br>
<br>
When displayed to DS9 from IRAF, DS9 doesn't show the actual pixel values but shows an approximation based on transforming the z1/z2 values to the 200 grey level used in the display.  You might try to display with "ztrans=none"<br>
<br>
<blockquote>How can I change a mask into a textfile in order to check/change the pixel values? </blockquote><br>
<br>
Pixel masks are treated just like images, so tasks like LISTPIX an EPIX will work.  Hope this helps.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
