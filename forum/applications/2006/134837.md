Please post more about how you're trying to do the corrections, i.e. is it just CCDPROC (what are the params?) or are you doing something like calling imarith/imexpr directly?<br>
<br>
&gt; Also I was wondering whether imstat can be used to get the value at a point?<br>
<br>
You could use image sections, e.g.<br>
<br>
    cl&gt; imstat dev$pix[128,256]<br>
<br>
will operate only on pixel (128,256).  The statistics of one point are kinda silly, note you can use LISTPIXEL on the same section if all you want is the pixel value.<br>
Trying to use this trick in a script to operate on entire images is also kinda silly, don't bother.<br>
<br>
-Mike
