                For anyone curious, I did it in this manner.<br>
<br>
1. create a file, in my case "expr", with this inside <pre><code> ((sqrt((I-256)**2 + (J-256)**2) &lt;= 50) ? a : -1) </code></pre><br>
<br>
2. then, I ran this <pre><code> imexpr @expr statimg M13Irefnew1.fits </code></pre><br>
<br>
3. finally, <pre><code> imstat statimg.fits lower=0 </code></pre><br>
