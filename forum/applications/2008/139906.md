<div>
I am not sure how this task is implemented.  However, you can get the log format in two steps.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
:yaxis log
:/ytrans log
[now cause a new fit or redraw]
</code></pre><br>
<br>
The / commands are document either with "help gtools" or ":/help" in graphics mode.<br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
