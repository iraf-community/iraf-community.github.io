<div>
Hi Ricardo,<br>
<br>
That does seem mysterious.  The command msctpeak executes is:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
imcentroid &lt;image&gt; reference=&quot;&quot; &lt;coords&gt; &lt;shifts&gt; &lt;boxsize&gt; verb+
</code></pre><br>
<br>
where it supplies the parameters in the angle brackets.  Any other hidden parameters take their current setting.  So you can influence the centering with those.  The safest thing to do is "unlearn imcentroid".  If that still fails and you've adjusted the boxsize in the msctpeak parameters then I have no idea what is wrong and I'd have to try and reproduce the problem with your data.<br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
