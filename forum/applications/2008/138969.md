**James Turner** wrote on Jan 30, 2008

{% raw %}
<div>
I did something like this in IRAF using "IDBVIP" and other associated ForTran subroutines, obtained from Starlink PDA library (but originally from TOMS). The result is a smooth piecewise interpolation, rather than a Delaunay triangulation.<br>
<br>
  <a href="http://www.starlink.rl.ac.uk/star/docs/sun194.htx/sun194.html">http://www.starlink.rl.ac.uk/star/docs/sun194.htx/sun194.html</a><br>
<br>
This involves writing your own (relatively simple) IRAF task in SPP, however, and linking to the library. My task was for 3D datacubes rather than simple images.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
<br>
James.
</div>
{% endraw %}
