<div>
NIRICLEAN is probably just a script task, and since it has parameters would be declared as<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cl&gt; task niriclean = gemini$niri/niriclean.cl
</code></pre><br>
<br>
To make this a part of the NIRI package, put that command in the gemini$niri/niri.cl file, otherwise you can declare it in your login.cl/loginuser.cl before the final 'keep' statement.<br>
<br>
-Mike
