Hi Phil,<br>
<br>
I also feel like it has been ages since I've dealt with this type of question.  I had always hoped to add some special software and package for multislit.<br>
<br>
Anyway,  the way I would suggest is you keep the multispec format.  When you do identify on one, the middle or first for instance, then you use the keys to move to the next spectrum (j and k as I recall).  The lines and solution from the last one will still be present.  If you recognize the shift you could do a a recenter (probably recenter all).  At the end that shifted into the region you will be missing lines.  You can add them with 'm' or probably better would be a new find lines.  After getting the lines you would, of course, do a new fit.  After you've done one comp tediously, any other comps will be a matter of reidentify (for the same mask).<br>
<br>
I hope this slightly sketching description is helpful.<br>
<br>
Regards,<br>
Frank
