<div>
You need to disable the 'verify' APPHOT package parameter to avoid the prompt, i.e.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; apphot.verify = no</code></pre><br>
<br>
or else just 'epar apphot' to set it.<br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
