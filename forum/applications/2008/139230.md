**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Mar 25, 2008

{% raw %}
<div>
The various prompts are for the different parameters that can be fit, in your case the center position and width of each of the blended gaussians.  A value of 'fixed' means that you want to fix the value at the initial setting and not let it vary (e.g. to nail down the line position).  A 'single' means it can vary by a single offset, e.g. the doppler shift as a constant offset to each of the features.  'All' simply means to fit that parameter independently for each feature (e.g. the center position you marked it just a starting guess, find the fitted value).<br>
<br>
Hope this helps,<br>
-Mike
