Hello Antonio,<br>
<br>
I'm afraid that the way an "overscan" is defined and used by ccdproc is the entire column matched to the data.  You can put in an image section but the second part of the section is ignored. So your options are to determine the bias value separately and either subtract it (the constant) with imarith/imexpr or replace the entire column you are using right now by that value with imreplace and a section.<br>
<br>
Let me know if you want more suggestions.<br>
<br>
Yours,<br>
Frank Valdes
