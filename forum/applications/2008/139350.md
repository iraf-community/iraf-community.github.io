What is the error message?  Are these the same trimmed images you wrote about before or are they even spectra?<br>
<br>
You should see a prompt on the graphics window status line when you hit '#' where you enter the line number.  If your terminal type is set wrong this may be appearing in the text window, and depending on your X11 policy you may need to click on the text window to enter a value.  If these are trimmed images then line 800 may not be what you think it is if the WCS is still tied to the original image.  You can try "cl&gt; wcsreset image.fits wcs=physical" to reset this, I'd recommend working on a copy of the image as a first test.<br>
