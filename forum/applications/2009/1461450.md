The parameters you want are nerrsample, sigma0, and nerrsample.  Read the help for a description of the parameters and the algorithm.  Some hints, sigma0 is basically the standard deviation of a clean area of the continuum.  The invgain is harder to determine and you may just want to skip using it and assume the pixel uncertainties are independent of signal level.<br>
