<div>
The MAGNIFY task can arbitrarily scale an image and gives you a choice of interpolants.  If you want a brute force method, something like BLKREP will replicate pixels so you could e.g. expand as 2x2 then use IMARITH to divide each pixel by 4 to conserve the overall flux.<br>
<br>
-Mike
