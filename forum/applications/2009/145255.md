**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jan 15, 2009

<div>
If I understand your question, then no, I can't think of any task that does specifically that.  The FCONVOLVE task in the STSDAS.FOURIER package will allow you to convolve two images together.  If you're asking because the spectra have a non-linear dispersion then you might look at REBIN or SCOPY as a way to linearize the dispersion (probably a log-linear is what you want) to see if a fixed-width gaussian convolution could then be used.<br>
<br>
Please post more info about why you need this if you still have questions, although I'll probably punt to Frank for the expert answer at this point.<br>
