Hi Lorrie,<br>
Since I had the same difficulties at the beginning, probably you have the same problem I had: Identify the difference between Dark frames and Flats-Off. Seems that the Flats-off can be use "as darks" in some sense and they don't generate the errors with the mdf that you're getting. Check your Flat-images to find which ones are Flats-On (=Flats) and which are Flats-Off (=Darks).<br>
