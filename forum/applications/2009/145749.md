<div>
You need to use the 'compute mode' syntax, i.e.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>files (&quot;*.fit&quot;, &gt; fitfile)</code></pre><br>
<br>
What you have now is 'command mode', useful on the commandline or when running scripts via indirection.<br>
<br>
-Mike
