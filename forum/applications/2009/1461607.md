<div>
See tasks in the TTOOLS package of TABLES, specifically TCALC will let you create a new column by applying a correction to the MAG column.  Tasks like TEDIT will let you do other edits, but .als files are just text tables.  You might also check the photometry cookbooks, I seem to remember something about aperture correction but don't quite remember the details.<br>
