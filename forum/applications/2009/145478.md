<div>
<blockquote>imexpr expr = "((a &gt; 9.4) ? ) 1:0)", output = Badpixels.fits, a = Hotpixels.fits</blockquote><br>
<br>
1) The expression has an extra right paren, should be "((a &gt; 9.4) ? 1 : 0)"<br>
2) No commas between the arguments unless you are in 'compute mode' (i.e. "imexpr(expr='a', a=foo, b=bar, ....)")<br>
<br>
-Mike
