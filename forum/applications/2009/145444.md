Sorry for overlooking this.  On the chance you didn't find a solution, the trick in your case is to quote the label string with single quotes, then the double-quote for the arcsec will be taken literally.  For example,<br>
<br>
<pre><code>xlabel &#x27;\\iSeparation (&quot;)&#x27;</code></pre><br>
<br>
I'm not sure there's a way to use <strong>both</strong> single and double quotes in the same label, however.<br>
<br>
-Mike
