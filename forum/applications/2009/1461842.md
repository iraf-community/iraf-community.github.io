ecl&gt; imhead tempb<br>
tempb[1052,1028][real]: Westerlund1-N<br>

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 18, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
Hi Phil,<br>
<br>
The '[' and ']' chars are special to the filename template, to append a section to an @file you need to escape it.  For example<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; imcopy @z//\[96:1052,165:1020] tempb</code></pre><br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
{% endraw %}

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Jun 18, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
Try a backslash in front of the comma in the section as well, i.e.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>imcopy @z//\[96:1052\,165:1020] tempb</code></pre><br>
<br>
If you want to test out the template expansion like this you can use the SECTIONS task.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>
