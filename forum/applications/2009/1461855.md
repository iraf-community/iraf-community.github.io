<div>
You need to do an<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; unlearn hselect</code></pre><br>
<br>
The 'missing' parameter was recently added but you're still using old parameters from your uparm directory.  To avoid similar problems in the future, you can do a new MKIRAF and reinitialize your uparm directory.  Otherwise, unlearning the offending task will fix most 'parameter not found' messages.<br>
<br>
Also, I've put MSCRED v4.9 macintel binaries in the ftp archive on iraf.noao.edu, you can also check the downloads link on this site.<br>
<br>
-Mike
