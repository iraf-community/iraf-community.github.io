Daryl Willmarth just showed me something very similar.  I was slightly swayed by your suggestion of a tracing problem.  But now I think the more likely cause, because I've seen something like this years ago, is that the FITS file is corrupted in the sense that it is jumping into the middle of the first spectrum (because the offset from the header to the data is off by a FITS "block") and what you are seeing is a wraparound into the next aperture.<br>
<br>
So I will be looking at why this happens with Daryl's example.<br>
