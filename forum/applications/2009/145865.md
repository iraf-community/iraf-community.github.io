**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 01, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
Readcor is used in cases where the zero level bias is dependent on the readout.  In this case a 2-D zero image it will be averaged across the readout axis specified by the 'readaxis' parameter.  Whether you need this depends on the nature of the detector, or whether you observe in "longscan" mode (i.e. a continuous readout to produce an arbitrarily long strip).<br>
<br>
Unless you know the detector requires it, turn it off.<br>
<br>
-Mike
</div>

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 01, 2009

{% raw %}
<div>
'Scancor' refers to an observing mode more than the detector specifically.  In most cases the CCD is exposed for some time while the telescope tracks the object, in "scan mode" the CCD is clocked to read out continuously to produce a "strip" that may be longer than the physical detector.  This might be used to e.g. observe a fast-moving minor planet or a transit observation for some reason.
</div>
{% endraw %}
