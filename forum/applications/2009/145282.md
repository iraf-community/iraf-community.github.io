Hate to say it, but it works for me:<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
ecl&gt; cd /tmp
ecl&gt; imcopy dev$pix test.fits
dev$pix -&gt; test.fits
ecl&gt; hfix test.fits
     ....then I just add the HIERARCH keywords from your post
ecl&gt; esoselect *.fits &quot;HIERARCH ESO TPL NEXP&quot; 6
test.fits
ecl&gt; esoselect *.fits &quot;HIERARCH ESO INS FILT1 NAME&quot; R_SPECIAL
test.fits
ecl&gt; esoselect *.fits &quot;HIERARCH ESO INS FILT1 NAME&quot; I_BESS 
ecl&gt;
</code></pre><br>
<br>
Maybe we're just using different versions of the script.  I've put mine at <a href="http://iraf.net/ftp/pub/esoselect.cl">http://iraf.net/ftp/pub/esoselect.cl</a> in case you want to give that a try.<br>
