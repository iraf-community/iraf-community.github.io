Hi, Frank---<br>
<br>
A few comments on your parameters...<br>
<br>
The width parameter is used only by the centering algorithm, and should be<br>
"base to base".  I made mine a little smaller than yours because there isn't<br>
clean separation between different fibers.  The nice thing about using<br>
the treashold parameter is that it stops it from finding a bunch of fibers that<br>
wasn't assigned.  I see you get around this by specifying nfibs=29 but then your numbering must not agree with the id's in the header. Using 141 results in most of them being numbered correctly.<br>
<br>
Also, it's harmless but redundant to specify weighting=variance if you have clean set to yes.  <br>
<br>
My experience is that a 1st order cubic spline is needed for a good comparison solution.<br>
<br>
Someone really should write a manual for all of this....<br>
<br>
But the behavior described above (two apertures being identified for a single<br>
one) is clearly some sort of bug, and it may be affecting other things as<br>
well.<br>
<br>
Thanks!<br>
<br>
---phil
