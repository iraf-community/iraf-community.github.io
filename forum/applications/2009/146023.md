<br>

<br>
for (i=1; i&lt;=5; i+=1) {<br>

<div>
See the FIELDS task, it'll let you select columns from a text file over a range of rows.  The 'undefined variable' message likely means the scan into the variable failed, e.g. 'filename' isn't a struct variable or you read past the end of the input.<br>
<br>
-Mike

**Mike Fitzpatrick** wrote on Apr 08, 2009

<div>
Try<br>
<br>
<pre><code>cl&gt; fields myfile.txt &quot;1,2&quot; &quot;1-5&quot; &gt; newfile.txt</code></pre><br>
<br>
Will write columns 1 and 2 from the first 5 lines to 'newfile.txt'
