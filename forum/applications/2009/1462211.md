<div>
Wouldn't you know it, it was something very simple.  I was not resetting the datamin value in the script for each individual image.  When datamin was above the value of the minimum of the psf function the look-up table would not write.  (Possibly it wouldn't converge?) When I corrected this, psf stopped hanging.  (I think I wrote that correctly.)<br>
<br>
I checked the well depth of the CCD that OGLE used.  Its over 100 000 electrons.  So, even a high psf would be acceptable and realistic in the data for a bright star.
