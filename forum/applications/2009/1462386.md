<div>
You are right that the values to CRMEDIAN are hardwired into the CRNEBULA script.  The simplest way to increase the size is to make your own copy of the  script and edit the change, e.g.<br>
<br>
<pre><code>
cl&gt; copy crutil$crnebula.cl home$mycrnebula.cl
cl&gt; edit home$mycrnebula.cl
    .... change the 25 to 200
cl&gt; task mycrnebula  = home$mycrnebula.cl
</code></pre><br>
<br>
Frank will see this and may decide to make these values changeable as parameters for a future release. <br>
<br>
Cheers,<br>
-Mike
</div>
