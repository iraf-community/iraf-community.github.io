<div>
I was hoping somebody with more experience in this would reply, but an idea you might consider is simply copying your image in to a larger blank image so there are enough zero pixels on the border to include your radius.  This would allow the radius to extend beyond your data without worrying about boundary problems without contributing any signal to the aperture.  The MKPATTERN task in ARTDATA can create a blank image, and you can IMCOPY the image into to blank data using sections.  Hope this helps.<br>
<br>
-Mike
