<div>
One way this might happen is if your display frame buffer isn't big enough for the image, or if you display the image with the 'fill' parameter turned on.  TVMARK works off of pixel coords, so long as you don't  pisition the image other than in the center of the frame buffer or scale it somehow and assuming the coords are correct, the circles should line up.<br>
<br>
-Mike
