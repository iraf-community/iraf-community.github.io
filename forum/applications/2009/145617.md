<div>
A '$' in front of the task name when you declare it means that the task as no parameters, and so you don't see the prompt.  Try declaring as<br>
<br>
<pre><code>task prueba = /home/iraf/noao/casleored/prueba.cl</code></pre><br>
<br>
-Mike
