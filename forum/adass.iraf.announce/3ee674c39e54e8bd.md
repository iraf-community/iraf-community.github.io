## Japan FTP/Web mirror

**Ichikawa Shin-ichi** wrote on Jan 16, 1998

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">



IRAF Web/FTP Mirror now available at NAOJ/ADAC

Now we open a mirror of the NOAO IRAF ftp directories as well as
the IRAF web pages. The IRAF FTP archive is available at
http://sinobu.mtk.nao.ac.jp/iraf/ftp or ftp://sinobu.mtk.nao.ac.jp/NOAO.
The web pages, which has been opened for a year, can be accessed at
http://sinobu.mtk.nao.ac.jp/iraf/web.

We hope that our IRAF mirror will be useful to Japanese and
East Asian users.
