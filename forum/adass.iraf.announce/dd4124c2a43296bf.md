## Australian IRAF mirror

**Bill Roberts** wrote on Oct 10, 1999

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">
An Australian mirror of the IRAF Web site is now available at:

http://iraf.anu.edu.au/

A mirror of the ftp site is available at:

ftp://www.mso.anu.edu.au/iraf/

Due to disk space limitations, only the most recent version of the IRAF
software is available at this site.

The Australian IRAF mirror is hosted by the Research School of Astronomy
and Astrophysics, Australian National University.
