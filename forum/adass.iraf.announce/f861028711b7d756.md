## New issue of IRAF Newsletter

**IRAF Newsletter** wrote on May 19, 1998

<pre style="background: transparent; box-shadow: none; border: none;">


To All IRAF Users:

A new IRAF Newsletter is now available documenting major features of the
IRAF V2.11 release, as well as other recent IRAF-related software
developments from both NOAO/IRAF and various outside groups.

The newsletter is available both electronically on the Internet, and in
printed form via the mail.  The electronic version is available now using
any of the following forms:

  WWW:          http://iraf.noao.edu/irafnews/apr98
  PDF:          ftp://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/docs/newslet_14.pdf
  Postscript:   ftp://iraf.noao.edu/iraf/docs/newslet_14.ps.gz

If you would prefer to receive a printed copy of this and future IRAF
Newsletters, --&gt; simply reply to this email &lt;-- and include your current
postal mail address either explicitly or as your .signature trailer
(alternatively one can email irafnews@iraf.noao.edu at any time to get on
the printed newsletter subscription list).  All registered IRAF users will
receive electronic notification of this and future IRAF Newsletters, but
as our mailing list is out of date it is necessary to send us your address
again if you wish to receive printed copies of the Newsletter.

We are distributing paper copies of this Newsletter to roughly 60 libraries.
If you would like your library to be on our postal mailing list please
send us (irafnews@iraf.noao.edu) the postal mail address of your library.
To check whether your library is already subscribed please see

  Libraries:    http://iraf.noao.edu/irafnews/librarylist.html
 
Doug Tody
Jeannette Barnes
</pre>
